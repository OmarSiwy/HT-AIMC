# N6 round 2 (attack A1): conversions, fewer, faster, cheaper

2026-10-06. Owner: A1 (round-2 converter attack). This is revision **2b**, after the adversarial critic. Revision 2a
is kept in the scratchpad (`<scratchpad>/a1r2/N6_r2_round2a.md`), and its headline numbers are **withdrawn** (§0).

Model: `scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n6_readout.py` (opt-in `t_model="meas"` path and the `r2_*`
options). The critic fixes touch only the `meas` path: 2,700 `adc()` calls on every round-1 option are
byte-identical before and after. Designs: `scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/designs/r2_A1_*.json`. Each one
carries `scores_projected` for every frame. Scratch work: `<scratchpad>/a1/` (2a decks and searches) and
`<scratchpad>/a1r2/` (2b: `sp2.py` ESPice, `r2b.py` family search, `cross.py`, `final2.py`, `writej.py`).

Labels: **M** measured (ESPice ASAP7 TT 27 C here), **D** derived (a law applied to measured numbers), **P**
projected (literature or law only). Every tok/s, TOPS/W, tok/W and tok/J on this page is **P**, because the
system model is a stack of laws.

Frames. **Shared** = the evaluator as it stands (`notes_native.score_design` joint frame: KV 4/8 + 16 MB stack,
with N9 charging its reference per n_adc slot). **Fixed** = the same with the scratch N9 patch I1 (reference energy
per physical conversion, buffer area per physical converter). **CLI** = `cli.py` core frame (no notes_native or
N9 hooks). Per the critic, the **shared reading is the headline** until N9 lands I1 in the evaluator.

## 0. Bottom line (revision 2b)

1. **Loud correction: the 2a lead's +30 % is withdrawn.** Five critic findings were correct and are now in the
   model: (i) the pool-bus wire on the pooled node, (ii) FIA area from the measured device, (iii) the FIA switch
   drive missing from κ, (iv) the broadcast of each decision to the K tiles' DAC drivers, and (v) back-end timing
   and energy. Under them the 2a lead design (RVT 450 ps, K 4, share 4, cu 1.0 fF) is **infeasible**: G2 −62 dB,
   because the quiet comparator no longer fits on the attenuated node. The 2a upper bound (K 8, 4-track bus) is
   infeasible for the same reason.
2. **New ARCH lead `r2_A1_best` (re-searched under the corrected model):** the same architecture (a pipelined
   residue-amp SAR on K = 4 charge-pooled tiles, 2-track center-tapped bus, monotonic DAC, 2-b flash seed),
   re-sized: LVT 150 ps FIA, adc_share 6, 3 bits/cycle, 11 b, cu 1.25 fF.
   **Shared: 60,835 / 11.22 / 629 / 643** (ARCH), 80,753 / 10.89 (Sohu). Fixed: 67,426 / 10.47 / 593 / 593.
   CLI: **36,844 / 11.30**.
3. **Against round 1, stated honestly.**
   - It **loses** to the round-1 pick *as scored*: 67,760 / 11.25 shared, so −10 % tok/s (fixed: −2 %).
   - That as-scored number is itself overclaimed: its converter timing was about 2x optimistic. Re-priced on the
     same corrected parts, the round-1 converter reads only **19,790 / 3.55 shared and 29,372 / 4.99 fixed**.
     Against that honest round-1 pick the lead is 3.1x shared and 2.3x fixed.
   - In the CLI core frame the lead also loses to the pick: 36,844 against 48,831 (−25 %). The lead was designed
     in the joint frame. The CLI omits the KV 4/8, slice-merge and N9 hooks, and the pick's converter there is
     the unmeasured FO4-law one.
4. **Against the systolic baselines (lever-matched, ARCH):** the shared reading is 60,835 against the literature
   PE frame's 59,242. That is **+2.7 %, i.e. parity within the model's uncertainty, at 0.61x the TOPS/W** (11.22
   against 18.31). Against the ppa PE frame (50,017 / 7.74) it is +22 % and 1.45x TOPS/W. Under Sohu the Sohu lead
   (82,764 shared) **loses** to the literature PE frame (86,229) by 4 %.
   Any tok/s claim over digital is conditional on four things: V2 (the 1:16 slice merge, −36 % without it), the
   +3 dB ADC class weight (without it the lead is infeasible at −0.49 dB), N9 I1, and SS/FF closure.
5. **Sohu lead `r2_A1_best_sohu`:** the same converter with the lower-κ LVT 300 ps FIA, adc_share 4 and 2
   bits/cycle. Sohu: 82,764 / 11.81 shared and 80,753 / 11.26 fixed, against the pick's 72,220 (+15 % / +12 %).
   The 2a Sohu lead (K 8, share 1) existed only through the N9 bug and the ignored bus wire: it reads 43,477 shared.
6. **The 12-b ≥ 635 MS/s ≤ 254 fJ converter is still not needed.** The lead runs one 11-b physical conversion per
   1.06 to 1.13 ns slot. The front stage (coarse SAR + bus + broadcast + RA window) takes 0.99 ns and the back
   stage (7 + 1 b after a 9.5x gain) 0.44 ns. It spends **947 fJ per physical conversion at 9.53 ENOB**, which
   gives a Schreier FoM of **176.4 dB, sampler excluded** (the compute column is the sampler, and no input driver
   or sampler kT/C is charged). With the N9 reference share (550 fJ) it is **174.4 dB**. Both sit inside the
   published high-speed envelope, not past it.
7. **The corners are still open, and the converter is not what blocks them.** The lead fails SS by −1.32 dB and
   FF by −0.95 dB. Larger cu (1.25 fF) helps compared with 2a's −1.99 / −1.56. `r2_A1_corner` (the runner-up
   stack + the A1 converter, K 8 / share 1, cu 1.75) passes SS +0.04 and FF +0.37 at 60,005 fixed (−11 %). In the
   shared frame it reads 17,362, because K 8 / share 1 is the most I1-sensitive point.

## 3. Model (n6_readout.py, `t_model="meas"`)

    κ_eff   = (sqrt(γ κ_amp) + sqrt(κ_SA)/A)^2 · (kT/kT_300) · (VDD/0.7)        (D; κ_amp incl. switch drive, §2e)
    g       = G · g_bus · g_in
    g_bus   = C_col / (C_col + c_w·K·pitch·(1 + 0.25 (tracks − 1)) − n5's share)  (critic #3)
    C_in    = 4kTγ / (share · (σ_q g)^2 · (A·Co/C_in));  g_in = C_col/(C_col + C_in)   (fixed point; no fit if C_in ≥ 3 C_col)
    area    = (C_in / 1.914 fF) · (200 fF + 168 fins · Cgg) / ρ_MOS + cheap comparators, trims, DAC, logic   (critic #2)
    t_loop  = 2 FO4_lib + τ_DAC (6.9 + 0.72 (b_i − 6)) + 2 FO4 + t_bc,  t_bc = 2 FO4_lib + 0.38 r (K pitch/2)^2 c   (critic #7)
    Gr      = min(ra_gain, 0.38 VDD/(2^(nv+1) Δ g), A_FIA)                        (critic #4)
    back    = n_bc · (t_SA,imc(σ_be) + t_loop(no t_bc) + FO4_lib),  σ_be = Gr g σ_q; energy on the κ_SA law + 0.5 C_o V^2
    t_conv  = max(t_front, t_back + t_window) + min(t_front, t_back)/rounds       (pipe_ra; critic #11)
    + async logic 20 gate toggles per cycle and broadcast (2^bpc − 1) C_bc V^2 per front cycle   (critic #9)

Unchanged from 2a: monotonic (detect-then-inject) packets with variance kT·v_eff/(VDD·C_col); the reference term;
P_meta = 2e-11 per conversion (derived from 19n to 19n3, costs 69 ps); and the bpc, n_red, flash-seed and
dac_density knobs.

**Why float or log readout does not save energy (D, unchanged).** Under an MSE gate, minimising E[1/σ(x)²]
subject to E[σ(x)²] = N gives a constant σ (Lagrange). Range-adaptive early termination is a time lever only
(P, unscored).

## 7. Interactions

- **I1, N9 reference accounting (bug, highest impact).** N9 sizes the reference buffer and charges reference energy
  per n_adc slot. Physically, area goes with physical converters (n_adc/amort) and energy with physical
  conversions, where amort = K x slices (`phys_adc_frac`). **No design in the A1 space is invariant to it.** The
  lead moves +11 % tok/s / −7 % TOPS/W, K 2 / share 6 moves −4 %, and K 8 / share 1 moves 2 to 3.5x. The patch
  is in `<scratchpad>/a1/n9fix.py`. It must land in N9 before any pooled design is ranked in one frame.
- **I2, FO4.** n6 `meas` runs its logic at the liberty FO4. n2's t_word, n3's t_row and n9's slot still use the bare
  FO4. The lead is t_word-bound (5.66 ns word, t_pass 6.78 ns).
- **I3, N3/N5 corners.** §6.
- **I4, N5/N7 mapping.** K = 4 needs the 4 row tiles that feed the same output columns stacked around a center
  converter, with a 2-track M6-class bus per pooled column and a distributed CDAC with a decision broadcast. The
  bus wire is on the pooled node, so N5 should count it once, here, and not in `c_in_fF` (it does not, unless
  `c_in_mode="direct"`).
- **I5, N8.** The lead keeps the unmeasured +3 dB ADC class weight. `r2_A1_nocredit` is the fallback. The harness
  run (converter-like uniform noise at 9.53 ENOB over ±32σ) decides between them.
- **I6, N4/V2.** −36 % without the 1:16 slice merge.
- **I7, N9 reference noise.** The packet and reference terms (126 µV each) are comparable to σ_q (182 µV). A
  rail-referred ratiometric DAC would remove the reference term.
- **I8, test suite.** The default-design FAIL is upstream of n6 (§1 #12).
