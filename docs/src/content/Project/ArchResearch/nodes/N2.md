# N2: Compute domain

2026-10-05, revision 3 (after two adversarial critics). Owner module:
`scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n2_domain.py`. Objective: [ARCH_METRIC.md](../../ARCH_METRIC.md).
Reproduce:

    cd scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes
    nix-shell -p 'python3.withPackages(p:[p.numpy p.scipy])' --run 'python3 n2_domain.py --hi'          # arch, 28 + 43 dB
    nix-shell -p 'python3.withPackages(p:[p.numpy p.scipy])' --run 'python3 n2_domain.py --hi --sohu'   # Sohu, W8

The script runs the self-check, prints the die area and the systolic baseline under the chosen
condition set, then the best design per option for each gate (and, for arch, streaming and resident
weights). The full `--hi` run takes about 30 minutes, single process. The side runs behind the "Analog
value-add" and "43 dB wall" tables (per-k hybrid, SRAM vs gain-cell storage, γ and PVT sensitivities,
best-margin designs) are scratchpad scripts (`n2_side.py`, `n2_wall.py`, `n2_cur.py`). They call only
`n2_domain.designs/evaluate/best`.

**Labels.** Every ARCH_METRIC number on this page is **projected**. Each one combines literature
constants and laws with measured ASAP7 device data (Ron and TG Ron, Cgg, FO4, gm/ID, Ioff, StrongARM
energy, gain-cell leakage) and the measured sky130 macro. One number was **measured** here: the
stacked-FinFET unit current (see "SPICE check"). No ASAP7 compute column has been simulated yet.

**What changed in revision 3.** Every point below changed numbers.

- The 43 dB grid is now keyed on the *analog* target, gate − relief. The old run clipped the hybrid to
  Cu ≥ 1 fF and B ≥ 8, which is where the refuted 30,885 came from.
- k_dig is searched over 1–5. k = P (all six planes digital) is now digital CIM by construction
  (self-check).
- All non-anchor options store their bits in live-N3 **gain-cell bits** (`gaincell_mom_caps`), not
  6T SRAM. ARCH_METRIC constraint 2 rules out SRAM bitcells in the tile.
- The rail-drive share switch is priced at the worst-case 1n1p TG Ron (18.4 kΩ, measured) instead of
  the V_S = 0 nmos value (5.95 kΩ). Its gate energy is added.
- Digital gating uses the liberty INV energy (0.655 fJ, not 0.121), and the adder-tree FA count is
  exact (309 FAs for 64 × 4 b).
- Bit-slice noise is recombined with 4^b weights.
- The current domain now has: integrated channel noise 2γkT·gm·T; t_q ≥ x·τ_row (x = 3 or 10) plus a
  PWM edge-error term; PVT modelled as a column gain trimmed on the checksum column (0.1–0.6 %);
  gm/ID down to 3; and the gain cell's own write kT/C.
- N8's 3.6 dB static-mismatch penalty is applied at the 43 dB gate.
- New options: `hybrid_wmsb_digital`, `charge_analog_weight`, `current_gaincell_cal`,
  `time_delay_cal`, and `cco_freq_mac`, which was reclassified and is now scored. C-2C gets a
  single-slice W8 mode.
- Every row now reports the 27n1 bit-normalized TOPS/W and the effective output bits.
- Within one option, designs pinned at the same HBM cap are treated as tied on tok/s (relative
  tolerance 1e-6). Before this, float noise picked the design.

Findings that matter more than the ranking:

1. **The king metric saturates, so N2 is ranked by TOPS/W.** Under streaming, tok/s per die is
   capped by decode HBM traffic at the KV limit: 39,693 (arch), 47,227 (arch, resident) and 90,598
   (Sohu W8, 1063.4 mm² die from `metric.sohu_area()`). The lead, digital CIM and the 28 dB current
   designs all sit on the cap. N2 can only stop being the bottleneck; N1 and N4 hold the levers on
   the cap (KV bits, weight bytes).
2. **At 43 dB every transistor-mismatch domain dies of area and time, not of an absolute wall.** The
   best-margin designs pass or come close only at 16–140 µm² per weight and 0.1–5 µs per pass. The
   per-stream floor then kills them (table "43 dB wall"). PVT calibration and gm/ID 3–4 do not
   rescue them, because the mismatch area is (gm/ID · A_VT/σ)² and σ must reach about 0.2–0.3 %.
   `time_delay` stops at a 40 dB jitter wall.
3. **The ARCH_METRIC log-domain hypothesis is falsified at 43 dB** (projected) by three
   independent terms. Each fails by itself: the PTAT temperature-slope residual (2 % → 34 dB,
   30.4 dB after the static penalty); the per-cell Vt trim (10 trim bits needed for 0.1 mV); and kT/C
   on the stored log weight (C_s = kT/(σ nU_T)² = 59 fF at 1 %, 234 fF at 0.5 %). At 28 dB it has the
   best TOPS/W (97) but loses tok/s (27.2k against the 39.7k cap).
4. **The 28 dB rows are not comparable with the exact baseline** (27n10 Q1): about 4.4 effective
   output bits, and N8 projects +32 % PPL. Treat them as an upper bound on analog, not as a
   quality result.
5. **No SRAM costs the lead 3–10 % TOPS/W.** The price is gain-cell refresh and crowbar leakage of
   22 nW per weight. At 43 dB: 35.2 with gain cells against 36.2 with 6T at equal k = 3; 32.9
   against 36.2 at each storage's lexicographic best. With 6T, k = 3 already reaches the cap.

## Scored options (frozen reference stack, best reachable design per option)

**Scoring regime.** Every node except N2 is its frozen shipped copy (`nodes/default/*`: the 27h1
SAR, the digital rail, ρ = 0.1, W4A8), except N3, which is the live gain-cell storage
(`gaincell_mom_caps`) for every option but the sky130 anchor. `n2_domain.evaluate()` wraps n5:
non-charge domains keep only n5's converter terms (adc, clip) and use their own error terms. It
also wraps n6, so a domain with its own readout (CCO, digital register) replaces the SAR. The live
node set scores every N2 option at 0 (W8A8 + 43 dB + ±32σ in the live N8, plus the live n5's
passive-share law). That is why `test_arch_eval.py` "default design" FAILs; even the exact digital
reference fails it (29.6 dB in the live n5), so no N2 change can fix it.

Search per option: rows 64–512, cols 64/128, ADC share 1–16, ADC bits 6–8 (8–12 when the analog
target is above 35 dB), Cu 0.2–4 fF (1–16 fF above 35 dB), plus the option's own knobs (share
fins, wire width, gm/ID 3–15, σ_u 0.6–5 %, PVT residual 0.1–0.6 %, t_q/τ_row 3–10, k_dig 1–5,
k_wbits 1–2, M, trim bits, …). Columns: tok/s/die, TOPS/W, tok/W, tok/J; **1b** = 27n1 TOPS/W ×
b_i b_w (× 32 at W4A8); **ENOB_out** = (SNR_eff − 1.76)/6.02, including the hybrid relief.
Systolic baseline (`baseline_systolic.py`, projected PE numbers, exact): **arch 30,806 / 7.53 /
382.5 / 694.9; Sohu 62,571 / 10.81 / 71.9 / 124.3.**

## Models (equations, units)

Common: P = input planes per word (6 for A8 with MSB skipping), R = rows, ρ = normalized rms of
one product (n5, 0.1), B = ADC bits, k_σ = converter range in σ, VDD in V, C in fF, t in ns, E in fJ
per crosspoint per word unless noted. The stack converts once per column per word (in-charge radix:
`model.tile` charges `phys` conversions per pass).

**Charge (passive share).** Signal σ_V = 2 V_exc ρ/√R, thermal 2kT/(R C_w) (27g2), so
SNR_th = 2 V_exc² ρ² C_w/kT, independent of R. Converter energy per MAC
k2 4^B VDD²/(256 V_exc² ρ²) (27h1). Rail drive (V_exc = VDD/2) quarters it. Delivery = sky130
anchor 4.50 fJ × (C/4 fF)(VDD/1.8)²(V_exc/(VDD/4))² × P/6. Plane slot (rail):
`slot = 3 (B+1) ln2 (τ_sw + τ_wire) × 1.2 + 4 FO4`, with τ_sw = Ron_TG/fins × R C_w, where
Ron_TG = 18.4 kΩ is the measured worst-case 1n1p TG over 0..VDD. The share node sits near V_cm,
where a lone nmos is 31 kΩ (measured), not 5.95 kΩ. τ_wire = ½ r L (R C_w + c L) (ORFS
32.3 Ω/µm, 0.173 fF/µm). Share-switch gate energy is P · 2 · 2·fins·Cgg · VDD²/R. Cu floor 0.2 fF
= `unit_cap_min_fF`. The cell switches' drain capacitance (≈ 2 × 11.3 aF per unit) sits on the
rail-driven bottom plate, so it does not enter the shared charge. It adds about 0.2 fJ/MAC of drive
energy (< 1 %, not modeled).

- **Bit-slice:** one unit per magnitude bit, c_col = R Cu. Recombining the bit columns
  with 2^b weights gives word noise Σ4^b = 21 equal column noises against 7 for binary caps at
  equal Cu (−4.8 dB). That is a thermal-equivalent signal ratio (2^m − 1)/√((4^m − 1)/3) = 1.53,
  for any weight distribution, replacing the refuted ×2. Per-column ADC ranges give back
  10 log10(E m²/Σ4^b p_b) = +1.1 dB (p_b = 0.50, 0.41, 0.13).
- **C-2C at W8:** one 7-b ladder (20 units, column sees 2 Cu, 6 serial sections), one slice.
- **Analog weight:** C_h = M C_u written by a column DAC. Each 1-plane samples V_w onto C_u.
  Errors: droop √(P−1)/(2M) (the spread of earlier samples; the mean is a per-plane gain that is
  trimmed); write kT/C_h relative to the rms weight voltage; DAC INL 0.3 %. Write energy
  (C_h + C_wl) VDD² + 20 fJ per weight per load. The column cap per weight is C_u, not 7 C_u.
- **OTA:** g = (V_out/k_σ)/σ_in, C_int = C_col/g, β = C_int/(C_int + C_col + C_par),
  E = VDD 2 C_L (B+1) ln2/(β gm/ID), ε = k_nl/(A0 β). The limit is OTA noise at a 0.15 V output
  swing, 4kT/(βC_L), not A0 (critic re-derivation: 29.8 dB at R64 Cu0.5).

**Current.** Unit = 1 fin × stack (L_eff = stack × 21 nm), σ_u = (gm/ID) A_VT/√(WL) (27g1, 27g4;
A_VT 1.3 mV·µm projected). I_u = IC(gm/ID) I_spec/stack (EKV, I_spec calibrated to 35.3 µA/fin). The
weight bits are gain-cell bits. Window T = (2^P − 1) t_q with t_q = max(0.1 ns, x τ_row), x ∈ {3, 10},
τ_row = ½ r c L² over the physical columns (wire plus one gate fin per unit). Errors:

- mismatch (E m²/E m)/σ_u²;
- gain cell: σ_u² + (gm/ID √(kT/C_s))² (the write kT/C);
- IR ε = gm_avg r_src R²/2 (15h1);
- kT/C_int plus integrated channel noise **2γ kT · R gm_avg · T** (γ = 1.0, long effective L);
- PWM edge ε = 0.3 τ_row √P/(½(2^P − 1) t_q);
- PVT: a column gain trimmed against the checksum column to 0.1–0.6 % (an 8×8 digital multiply per
  column per word, 64 e_FA/R).

Energy VDD(2 Q_avg + 2 I_clamp T) + reset 2 C_int V². TIA bias I_col 2^B/(V_read gm/ID): dead.

- **Program-verify (cal):** residual² = (gm/ID √(kT/C_s))² + σ_verify² (0.3 %) +
  (gm/ID · droop · 0.3)², where droop comes from the live N3 measured gate leakage over a 2 µs weight
  life. Write = 6 iterations × (C_s V² + 20 fJ DAC + comparator), 6 × 2 ns per row.
- **CCO:** count window T ≥ 2^B × 150 ps (live N6 t_LSB). No C_int, so the channel noise is
  referred to charge. Energy 2^B (5 e_INV,lib + 2 e_DFF) per column word. INL ±1 LSB after
  calibration (variance d²/4), area 4 × (ring + counter). The SAR is replaced through the n6 wrapper.

**Log (translinear).** I = I0 exp((V_w + V_x)/nU_T), with nU_T = 1/37.6 V⁻¹ measured. Errors:

- stored weight: σ_I/I = √(kT/C_s)/nU_T;
- row line: √(kT/C_row)/nU_T;
- Vt trim residual σ_trim/nU_T, with trim bits = ⌈log2(8 σ_Vt,1fin/(σ_trim √12))⌉: 8 b at 0.3 mV,
  10 b at 0.1 mV;
- shot noise;
- PTAT temperature-slope residual 2 % (a data-dependent exponent error, not a column gain, so the
  checksum trim does not remove it).

**Time.** Binary delay units (1 FO4 each) in ± chains, ring-oscillator TDC. Liberty INV energy per
stage. σ_t/t = α σ_Vt/(VDD − Vt). Jitter √stages × 1 % FO4. PVT is a trimmed column gain
(0.1–0.4 %).

- **cal:** cal_bits gain-cell trim bits plus a trim load per unit. These are set once per
  *physical* cell, so they are on-die and not streamed. The residual is 8 σ_raw/2^b/√12, and stage
  energy rises by 1.25×.

**Hybrids.**

- **Input planes (k_dig):** analog = charge_rail over P − k planes. Digital per crosspoint per
  digital plane = B_w e_INV,lib + F e_FA, with F = Σ_l (R/2^l)(B_w + l − 1)/R (4.83 at R64,
  B_w 4) and e_FA = 0.36 fJ. Area + 1.3 (B_w NAND + F FA). The tree reads the same gain-cell bits
  that steer the caps, so there is no second weight copy. Relief: the output-referred analog
  (data-independent) noise weight drops by (4^P − 1)/(4^(P−k) − 1) (18.1 dB at k = 3, 24.1 dB at
  k = 4). It holds for kT/C and ADC quantization in in-charge radix, and it is applied by lowering
  the analog target. Live n8 gets it through `q_credit_db`.
- **Weight bits (k_wbits):** the low magnitude bits are binary caps (3 Cu at k_wbits 1). A digital
  AND plus tree over (k_wbits + 1) bits runs on every plane. Relief = 10 log10(E m²/E m_low²) =
  3.0 dB at k_wbits 1 and 10.4 dB at 2 (INT4 half-normal, σ 7/3).

## Interactions

- **N1 / N4 own the tok/s cap.** Changing the domain moves only TOPS/W and the metrics below it.
- **N8 picks the domain.** At ≤ ~30 dB: current_gaincell if the user waives constraint 3,
  otherwise the hybrid. At 43 dB: only the hybrid among in-scope options, and digital CIM matches
  it. N8 should credit `k_dig`/`k_wbits` itself; N2 now passes `n2_relief_db` and adds it to
  `q_credit_db` in live mode. The ±32σ live range is untested for the hybrid's LSB-plane partials
  (open question 3).
- **N3.** All options except the anchor use gain-cell bits. The hybrid pays 22 nW/weight of
  refresh and crowbar leakage, 3–10 % of its TOPS/W. Bit-slice, C-2C, gain-cell, log, current
  and analog-weight cells are not N3 options; n2 overrides the cell in place (contract gap).
- **N5.** The accuracy law is a passive share; non-charge domains bring their own terms through
  `n2_domain.evaluate`. R64 is preferred: the TG Ron makes τ_sw ∝ R.
- **N6.** The charge domains' converter energy is the 27h1 r² term. A per-plane conversion with a
  ½-bit/plane taper would cut the 27h1 thermal term 2.06× (1.86× with integer bits) at equal
  quantization noise, but the stack converts once per word, so that saving is not available
  (see Critic responses). The time domain forces N6 = TDC, and CCO is an N6 readout that changes
  the N2 window.
- **N7.** The activation buffer (0.5 pJ/byte, about 19.5 fJ/MAC at R64) is the largest single
  energy term of the hybrid lead and of digital CIM. Most of the current domain's TOPS/W lead is
  buffer amortization at R128–R256.
- **N9.** VDD sweeps move Ron/TG Ron and FO4. A shorter hybrid word lets the die drop VDD at the
  cap (why k = 4 beats k = 1 at 28 dB).

## What a wrong choice costs

- Keeping the port (`charge`) instead of the lead: 3.3× fewer tok/s (12.0k vs 39.7k, 28 dB) and
  2.1× lower TOPS/W.
- Choosing current_gaincell (the 28 dB TOPS/W winner) if the true gate is 43 dB: the design does not
  exist at a usable area (19.5 µm²/weight, −2 dB). The hybrid keeps 39.7k / 32.9.
- Choosing the hybrid if the true gate is 28 dB: the same tok/s, but 2.4× lower TOPS/W than
  current_gaincell (32.9 vs 79.5). Without a constraint-3 waiver there is no better in-scope choice.
- Choosing the hybrid when digital CIM is allowed: about 0 ± 20 % at 43 dB (table above).
- Choosing log (the ARCH_METRIC hypothesis): −31 % tok/s at 28 dB, nothing at 43 dB.

## Falsifiers and SPICE/Verilog-A requests

- **Lead.** Falsified if a transient of the 2-plane analog LSB column fails to hold 18.9 dB
  (k = 4) or 24.9 dB (k = 3) in the 3-phase (B+1)ln2 slot. The transient should use R64, Cu
  0.2 fF MOM, the 1n1p TG cell switches with their drain capacitance, a 32-fin TG share switch,
  extracted column RC and B6. It is also falsified if a synthesized ASAP7 64-row × 4-b column
  adder tree costs > 2× (4.83 e_FA + 4 e_INV,lib) per crosspoint-plane.
- **Lead vs digital CIM.** Synthesize the same tree with and without the analog LSB column. If the
  analog column's (array + converter + leakage) energy per word exceeds the k = 5 → 6 digital-plane
  energy it replaces, the hybrid loses to digital CIM outright.
- **current_gaincell.** Falsified at 28 dB if the write kT/C plus read-device mismatch of a 1-fin
  gate-stored weight exceeds 1.5 % (σ), or if PWM edges cannot hold t_q = 3 τ_row over 129 columns.
- **log.** Revived only by a PTAT-tracking residual below 0.5 % across a 2 K gradient *and* a trim
  below 0.1 mV *and* a stored-weight noise below 0.13 mV per cell. Even with PVT at 0.5 %, it
  still scores 0 at 43 dB (`n2_side.py`).

## Open questions (for the core / other owners)

1. Core: let n2 return `arr["cell"]`/`arr["fmt"]`/readout overrides instead of mutating `cell`,
   `fmt` and `p["_adc_override"]` in place.
2. n5: when `arr["domain_parts_db"]` exists, use it plus the adc/clip terms. Otherwise current, time,
   log and digital are scored as passive shares. Live n5 gives the *exact* digital reference 29.6 dB,
   which is why the live default fails for every N2 option.
3. n8: credit `k_dig`/`k_wbits` itself, and measure on SmolLM2 tensors whether the hybrid's LSB-plane
   partials need ±32σ. If they do, B goes from 6 to 9.
4. User decision: is digital CIM in scope at the lossless gate? At 43 dB it equals or beats the
   analog lead (value-add table). Is constraint 3 waivable at a ≤ 30 dB gate (current_gaincell,
   79.5 TOPS/W)?
5. N1/N4: the streaming cap binds for every competitive domain. KV bits and weight bytes are the
   king-metric levers.
6. Projected constants that flip rankings: e_FA 0.36 fJ, the liberty INV, TG Ron at V_cm, γ, the jitter
   at 1 % FO4 per stage, the log PTAT residual of 2 %, and A_VT 1.3 mV·µm for stacked 1-fin devices.
   The first two decide hybrid vs digital CIM, so the deferred systolic and digital synthesis should
   include one 64-row adder-tree column.
