# N6: Readout and conversion

2026-10-05, revision 2. This revision follows two adversarial critic reviews; section 8 answers
every finding. Owner module: `scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n6_readout.py`.
Objective: [ARCH_METRIC.md](../../ARCH_METRIC.md), ranked tok/s per 100 mm² die (king), then
TOPS/W, then tok/W, then tok/J.

**Revision 2 in one paragraph.** The previous lead (`sar_vtc_fine`, 50.8 TOPS/W) priced its
continuous-time residue detector as one integrating window and its ramp competitor as 2^B
windows. That inconsistency is now fixed: both are priced with one law, n_ct = 3 windows. Under
that law the VTC lead falls to 38.2 TOPS/W, a tie with the plain redundant SAR. The new lead is
**`sar_direct_residue_amp`**, a critic-proposed missing option. It runs a noise-scaled coarse SAR
on the retained column, then one dynamic residue amplifier (sampled physics, the same Choo-class
FIA whose κ we already use), then a relaxed back end. Every option is now re-scored under
ARCH_METRIC's real conditions: weights stream from HBM, and ping-pong is off because REPO-A
falsified it. Four noise and cost terms that were missing are now priced: DAC packet kT/C,
reference noise and bias, kickback isolation, and auto-zero. **Under these conditions, analog
loses every metric to the systolic baseline at the N8-valid gates (F38, F44).** F44 is infeasible
for every readout. That loss comes from the cell and array (N3/N5) plus streaming, not from N6.

**Labels.** Every system number is **projected**: laws composed with measured ASAP7 device data
and literature constants. The comparator energies (0.44 fJ minimum StrongARM, 2.94 fJ IMC-grade)
and FO4 are **measured** with ESPice BSIM-CMG (`asap7_constants.json`). Their noise (7.5 mV and
1.24 mV) is **derived** from note 19p, because the ESPice transient-noise path is unbuilt, so no
SPICE was run for this node. Preamp κ is **projected** from Choo ISSCC 2021 (simulated). The
sky130 ports are **derived**. The quality harness is deferred, so `scripts/golden/quality/` was not
used. The gates come from N8 and the notes.

## 1. The law, corrected

```
Delta   = v_eff / 2^B,   v_eff = v_fs x range_mult / sqrt(K_pool)
window  E = max(E_min, kappa / sigma^2)                        one integrating noise window
CT detector (residue VTC, ramp) = n_ct windows, n_ct = 3        band-limited, fixed delay calibrated out
coarse SAR decisions: binary + 1 redundant cycle after decision m -> decisions 0..m may err by the
        redundant weight (absolute tolerance), later ones are quiet; m optimal. With a fine stage the
        doubled residue range lets every coarse decision err by 2^(nv-1) Delta.  sigma_i = max(sq, tol/4)
noise at the column:  Delta^2/12 + sq^2 + var_dac + var_ref + var_fine + droop^2/3 (+ stack / bridge)
   charge-injection packets  var_dac = 2kT v_eff/(VDD C_col)       (floated packets, trial + kept)
   bottom-plate CDAC         var_dac = kT/C_col (C_col/(C_col+C_dac))^-1 - kT/C_col,  C_dac = C_col v_eff/VDD
   buffered reference        var_ref = kT v_eff/(VDD C_col),  bias sum C_i (B+1) ln2 VDD/(gm/ID 15)
priced time: kickback isolation 2 FO4/decision, one auto-zero phase 10 FO4; trim 10 um^2 per class
```

## 3. Verdicts

**Lead: `sar_direct_residue_amp`** (24a1 pipelined-SAR on the retained node, 23d). The column caps
are the sampler. Charge-injection packets from a buffered reference null the column, and a
noise-scaled coarse SAR resolves B − 5 bits using two comparator classes. The doubled residue
range absorbs coarse errors up to 16 Δ. Then **one** dynamic residue amplifier (the
capacitor-biased FIA that defines κ_pre: one sampled, clocked window at the quiet noise) amplifies
the residue 16x. The 6 back-end decisions run at relaxed noise (1/G²) and are almost free. The
result is n_q ≈ 1.1, against 5.25 for the redundant SAR. At F28 that is **4.4x less converter
energy than the redundant SAR (0.58 against 2.58 pJ) and +40 % TOPS/W**, and at F38 +46 %
(1.69 against 1.16). tok/s ties the fast SARs.
- *Leads to:* N5 `adc_share` 2 (F28) to 8 (F38); no ping-pong needed. N8: the clip-free range is
  paid in C_col, not in converter bits. N3/N2/N9/N4: the DAC/reference noise term
  kT v_eff/(VDD C_col) is now the readout's second floor, so V_exc, ρ and cu are the levers.
- *Falsifier:* an ASAP7 sim of the FIA on a 0.9-14 pF retained node showing input-referred noise
  above 1.5x the κ_pre prediction, or gain drift after background calibration above 0.1 % (each
  brings it to within 10 % of `sar_vtc_fine`), or kickback through the isolation phase above Δ/4.
- *Confidence:* medium-low. The device is the one the κ comes from, which makes the sampled
  physics consistent, but κ is simulated literature and no ASAP7 transient noise exists.

**Viable.**
- `flash_sar_hybrid` and `sar_direct_redundant`: co-fallbacks, equal within model error (38.2 /
  36.2 TOPS/W F28, 1.18 / 1.16 F38). They need no amplifier.
- `sar_vtc_fine`: same TOPS/W as the fallbacks at n_ct = 3. It takes the lead's place only if a
  measured CT-detector κ·n_ct falls below about 1.5x one integrating window.
- `cco_per_column`: second on TOPS/W in F28 (46.8), but its 38 ns serial conversions cost 21 %
  tok/s without ping-pong. Dominated at F38, where 2^14 × 150 ps is too long.

**Dominated.** Pooling (exactly noise-neutral; bus RC costs tok/s), bottom-plate variants
(attenuation outweighs the packet term), ramps (time at ±32σ; the column-discharge ramp also pays
shot noise), ΔΣ and the pipelined shared converter (sampling from the retained node pays charge
sharing), the plain SAR, flash, the passive stack, the 27h1 fit, the sampled CDAC, and the sky130
integrator ports (dominated by time; they also fall below the gate at F38/F44 even with a
two-stage OTA).

**Infeasible.** The null-SAR sky130 port as ported, the 1-b sense amp, analog chaining, the TIA,
and the pipelined front sampler as sized.

## 4. Interactions

- **N8 (gate), dominant.** Readout energy ∝ SNR_adc; 28 → 44 dB costs about 40x per conversion.
  The ±32σ range now costs C_col through the DAC and reference noise.
- **N3/N5 (cu, R).** The DAC/reference term ∝ v_eff/C_col forces cu 8 → 16 fF at F38, and that
  area wall (with streaming) is what drops tok/s to 61. F44 is infeasible on the per-stream floor.
- **N2/N9 (V_exc).** E ∝ 1/V_exc² on the comparator term. The DAC/reference term falls as V_exc
  rises (smaller v_eff/C_col relative to the signal). A separate 0.45 V quiet rail gives +4 %
  TOPS/W at −3 % tok/s, so it is rejected by the king metric.
- **N4 (ρ, slices).** E ∝ 1/ρ². Two-slice W8 with analog merge gives +9 % TOPS/W. MSB skipping is
  negligible for the lead under any scaling.
- **N7.** No ping-pong is needed or assumed.
- **N9.** `n9_amp = "n6"`: the residue FIA is the κ_pre device and should be N9's first ASAP7
  noise characterization.

## 5. What a wrong choice here costs (F28 reference / F38)

- Shipping the 27h1 fit: same tok/s, TOPS/W ÷3.5 (F28) and ÷3.6 (F38).
- Keeping the revision 1 VTC lead: −25 % TOPS/W (F28) and −32 % (F38), at the same tok/s.
- CCO without a held column: −21 % tok/s (F28) and −61 % (F38).
- Porting the sky130 integrator chain: tok/s ÷4-5 (F28 streaming), infeasible at F38.
- At F44 nothing in N6 makes the design feasible.

## 7. Open questions

1. `model.tile` should pass `arr["c_col_fF"]` and the n5 attenuation to `adc()`. N6 recomputes
   C_col from the n3 default law, so the live gain-cell N3 is mis-sized (core change; not owned).
2. Self-test: everything passes except "default design: four finite positive metrics". The live
   stack (N2 hybrid, N3 gain cell, N4 w8a8_lead, N5 blk_diff, N8 bundle_lead) has `clip_ok`
   False (the N5 range against the N8 range requirement), so it fails regardless of the readout.
   An earlier failure, "no node fallbacks / no tile fits", was caused by N6 itself: the residue
   amp returned a 1e6 µm² sentinel on headroom overflow. It is fixed by clamping the gain.
3. N8 should publish a per-layer gate, so per-layer programmable resolution can be scored.
4. The frozen snapshot holds only SRAM cells. A non-SRAM default cell in `nodes/default/n3_cell`
   would make the absolute tok/s conform to ARCH_METRIC.
