# N10: Wildcards

2026-10-05, revised 21:30 after two critic reviews. Owner of
`scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n10_wildcards.py`. This node covers every architecture
idea N1-N9 do not own: spending devices on precision (27c9), pedestal removal (27e3), analog
dataflow between tiles (27l3), MoE (27l6), analog CAM for attention (27l9), iterative refinement
(28h1/28h4), Strassen-class bilinear algorithms (paper `sec_strassen`), unary and stochastic
computing, 3D stacking (27l8, `VERTICAL_3D.md`), self-speculative decoding, and everything else
the digests tagged N10.

**All numbers here are projected.** The tile each option rewrites is built from projected
constants and measured sky130 anchors, and the option laws come from the paper or the
literature. Inputs measured in this repo are marked [M]. Inputs derived exactly from a verified
decomposition are marked [D].

## Verdict in one paragraph

ARCH_METRIC fixes the dataflow as **streamed**, so the headline numbers are streaming numbers.
Resident numbers appear in brackets as an out-of-metric bound. **Provisional lead: `dps48`**
(DPS rank-48 bilinear lowering, one level). It is conditional on two things: (1) N3 provides a
continuous charge cell that writes and holds at least 7 b (the shipped `sram6t` INT4-slice cell
does not), and (2) the base design keeps a margin at least as large as the 0.13 dB algorithm tax.
Under those conditions it scores **12,922 tok/s/die, 16.1 TOPS/W, 648.7 tok/W** against
`none` at 11,905, 15.0 and 620.2: **+8.5 % tok/s, +7.5 % TOPS/W, +4.6 % tok/W**. Under the
Sohu conditions (streaming) the gain is +12 % (21,855 vs 19,517). These figures already charge
the B-side input-plane growth, the A-side combo forming and the extra write bits, which the first
version did not. On the shipped INT4-slice cell (measured F8 tax, median 7.5 dB) DPS-48 collapses
to 2,283 tok/s, so `none` remains the fallback. DPS-48 does not close the gap to the systolic
baseline: it reaches **0.44×** (29,150, frozen). Applying the same lowering to the baseline does
not help it (−4.4 % for DPS-48, +0.1 % for Strassen), because a PE must widen to the worst-case
operand growth. The bilinear upside is therefore specific to analog, but it is small. Every other
wildcard is dominated or infeasible. The live N8 gate (44 dB) scores every design 0, this one
included.

## Equations (units; constants from `asap7_constants.json` through the tile)

Per pass = one input vector through one tile. E_th and E_sw are the tile's 27h1 thermal and
switching converter energy per pass (J). t_w and t_c are the word and conversion times (ns).
Ping-pong gives t_pass = max(t_w, t_c). The frozen tile has t_w = 80.3 ns, t_c = 4.1 ns,
thermal 30.15 dB, mismatch 46.0 dB, ADC 34.9 dB, gate margin 0.81 dB.

- **Bilinear rank-R** (law:bilinear, `sec_strassen`), one level above tile granularity:
  passes × r_t = R/8 (Strassen 7/8; DPS-48 48/64; DPS-48⊗Strassen 0.656) and storage × r_s = R/4
  (7/4; 3; 5.25). Each stored tile sees r_t/r_s of the vectors, so e_pass and t_pass scale by
  r_t/r_s and tiles needed by r_s.
  - *B-side* (activations): a combo of b_src inputs grows the word by bx bits. Under n4's
    high-bit-skip plane schedule (6 planes per A8 word on average) the plane count follows the
    RMS growth ½ log2 b_src. That gives t_w and array energy × (planes + bx)/planes, with bx =
    1.12 (DPS-48) and 0.36 (Strassen), both [D] from the per-product nnz. The worst case
    (ceil log2, no skip) is 2.33 and 0.71.
  - *A-side* (weights): a combo of a_src sources needs ax = mean ceil log2 a_src extra stored
    bits (3.0 DPS-48, 0.71 Strassen [D]). Analog cell: write_bits_per_load × (w+ax)/w and the
    cell must resolve w+ax ≈ 7 b (an N3 property). INT4-slice cell: combos are rounded into
    4 b by a per-product shift, which is the F8 tax.
  - *Combo forming* (streaming; resident forms combos once): per stored element (a_src − 1)
    adds at e_elem (0.3 pJ, conservative for 4-7 b adds) plus a_src block-buffer reads at e_buf.
    This is folded into the per-bit write energy. DPS-48 a_src = 9.33 [D]. That is
    9.98e-5 J/token, 300× the plain write energy, and still under 7 % of token energy. Rail
    load: 2.2e12 adds/s/die against a 2e13 MAC/s rail (11 %, and attention does not bind).
    The source-block buffer (16 blocks, ~0.1 mm²) is not modelled.
  - *B-side and C-side adds*: (b_src − 1)·rows per input vector, shared by the 5,605/cols output
    tiles that reuse it (Llama-3-8B Σ K·N / Σ K [D]), and c_terms·cols per pass (DPS-48 7,
    Strassen 12/7 [D]).
  - *SNR tax* = algorithm tax + cell tax. Algorithm tax (paper Table, net random, projected):
    DPS-48 0.13 dB (repo-measured −0.02 dB algorithmically, STATUS DPS+LATTICE), Strassen 3.0 dB,
    DPS-48⊗Strassen 9.2 dB. Cell tax: 0 on an exact ≥7 b analog cell. On an INT4 slice it is
    F8 [M, sky130] +4.69..+10.93 dB with median 7.5 for DPS-48, and a projected 1.8 dB for Strassen
    (F8 median × 0.71/3 shift bits). The S1 bias path (18.1 dB, DPS-48) is assumed policed by a
    servo or ABFT, neither of which exists in this tile yet.
  - *Pay-off condition* (evaluator, frozen base, streaming): DPS-48 keeps all four gains while
    tax ≤ margin (0.81 dB). For 0.81 < tax ≤ ~1.6 dB, ADC 7→8 b pays the tax and keeps tok/s
    (12,903), but TOPS/W falls to 5.61 (−62 %), because the 27h1 converter energy is ∝ 4^B. Above
    ~1.7 dB the optimum moves to cu 1.5 fF and 128 rows (9,520, −20 % vs `none`). The cap lever
    costs area × cu and t_w ∝ C, because drivers are not upsized. Doubling cu therefore gives only
    +1.2-1.6 dB of total SNR, since the ADC part and the lower peak VDD absorb part of the
    thermal +3 dB.
- **Analog partial sums across K row-tiles** (27l3): charge-sharing K columns onto a bus of
  efficiency η gives V_c' = η V_c / √K. That assumes the converter range is rms/clip-set (N5's
  ±k σ); a worst-case full-scale range would not shrink. The 27h1 thermal term ∝ (VDD/V_c)² 4^B,
  so E_conv/pass = E_th/η² + E_sw/K. t_c per pass falls by 1/K, ADC area by 1/K, and t_w grows by
  1/planes per share slot. Gain part −20 log10(σ_g √K), with σ_g = 0.30 % [M] (EG_SERVO) and
  η = 0.84 [M] (CASCADE). The frozen tile is thermal 26,214 fJ vs switching 14.6 fJ per
  conversion, and t_w ≫ t_c, so even an ideal bus gains +0.8 %. The root cause is a ~4 mV column
  full scale. The lever that would change it is an amplified or active-integration column
  readout (N6).
- **Spending devices, residual** (27c9, Li 2025): W̄ = Σ γⁿ W⁽ⁿ⁾ with γ = 1/n_states, giving
  +20 log10(n_states) dB per tile on the mismatch part only, at × (1+N) cost. The tile is
  thermal-limited (30.15 vs 46.0 dB), so the gain is nil. Under N8's W8 floor the mismatch part
  (46 dB ≈ 7.3 b) becomes binding for W8, and this option must then be re-scored.
- **Spending devices, averaging** (27c9, Yousuf 2024): β copies give +10 log10 β dB on every
  random part, including the binding array kT/C, at β × area, conversions and passes. It is
  the only N10 lever on the thermal part. Its physical dominator is cu × β (N3/N5) with upsized
  column drivers: kT/C power ∝ 1/C at the same total cap, without β-fold conversions.
- **Pedestal** (27e3): f = 1/(1+TMR), bits lost log2(1+1/TMR) ≤ 1. A signed differential charge
  column has f = 0, so this is an exact no-op. It matters only for a unipolar N3 cell.
- **3D stacking** (27l8): silicon-honest accounting (every tier counts toward the iso-area die,
  ARCH_METRIC's "Die") gives ADC area / L, conversion rounds × L, plus a bond per physical column
  (pitch² = 9 µm², [P]). That is exactly `adc_share × L` plus bond area. Counting footprint would
  overclaim × L. ASAP7 has no BEOL compute device.
- **Residue IMC** (24a2, 28h1, 28m3): coarse pass, in-array subtract, amplify, fine pass, so
  passes × 2. The residue amplifier inherits the same small-swing (VDD/V_c)²·4^B noise penalty as
  the 27h1 comparator term; 28m3's 12 kT·4^B is only the floor. On top of that comes an OTA
  (79.1 pJ sky130 [M], ported VDD²). The subranging/pipelined converter form (subtract from the
  sampled column voltage, no array re-pass) is N6's choice and is **not** killed here.
- **Karatsuba**: the middle product (a_h + a_l)(b_h + b_l) grows both operands by one bit, which
  costs −12 dB at a fixed cell full scale. It is a no-op at W4 (one slice).
- **Unary / stochastic** (27h9): t_w × 2^{b_x}/planes (42.7× at A8), array energy × 14.112/2.449
  [M]. Stochastic streams take the same slots plus Bernoulli noise.
- **Factorization** (Xu 2025): storage × 2 and passes × 2. Its benefits do not apply to a
  defect-free cap cell.
- **Self-speculative decode** (28m8 draft, Leviathan 2023): τ = (1 − α^{k+1})/(1 − α). Cycle =
  k·T_draft(f_w W, f_kv KV, f_x pass time, B vectors) + T_verify(W, KV, B(k+1) vectors), with
  each T = max(compute, HBM, attention). f_x = f_w on an IMC tile (an MSB-plane draft runs fewer
  planes) and 1 on the systolic PE. Weight-pass energy scales by (P + G(k·f_x + k + 1)/τ)/(P + G),
  and streaming write energy follows loads: (1 + G(k·f_w + 1)/τ)/(1 + G).
- **MoE** (27l6): Llama-3-8B is dense (k/E = 1). Under streaming the k/E saving applies equally to
  any die (`MOE_MAPPING.md`).

## Interactions with other nodes

- **N3 decides the bilinear verdict.** `dps48` leads only on a continuous charge cell with ≥ 7 b
  write and storage precision. On the shipped INT4-slice cell it is `dps48_int4` (2,283 tok/s).
  The live N3 default (`gaincell_mos_caps`) is that kind of cell. N3 should state its write-DAC
  bits.
- **N8**: bilinear needs margin ≥ algorithm tax (0.13 dB DPS-48). Under the live 44 dB gate,
  every IMC design is 0. If N8 lands between 28 and 44 dB, re-run `study()`.
- **N4**: the B-side plane growth assumes the high-bit-skip schedule (RMS growth). Without plane
  skipping DPS-48 loses 3 % tok/s (worst-case 2.33 planes).
- **N5/N6**: `stack3d` = `adc_share × L`. `analog_psum` and `residue_imc` re-open only if N6
  moves to an amplified column range (switching-dominated converter).
- **N1**: streaming (fixed). Bilinear storage × r_s means more loads per wave, each shorter.
  Resident designs lose the ×3 storage under Sohu.
- **N7**: the rail carries 2.2e12 combo adds/s/die (11 % of its MAC rate) plus C-side adds.
- **Baseline**: bilinear does not help the systolic die (wide PE). Spec decoding does (up to
  ×1.50).

## What a wrong choice here costs

- `dps48` on the INT4-slice cell: −81 % tok/s (2,283 vs 11,905). Rejecting it on an analog cell
  forfeits +8.5 % tok/s and +7.5 % TOPS/W.
- `dps48` with 0.8-1.6 dB of excess tax: tok/s is kept by ADC +1 b, but TOPS/W drops 62 %.
- Spec decoding as an analog lever: −2 to −10 % tok/s on the streaming die, while it gives the
  baseline up to +50 %.
- 3D stacking: zero metric gain over 2D sharing, plus bonding, yield and a non-ASAP7 process.
- `analog_psum` K=8: −10 % tok/s and −24 % TOPS/W. Residual tiles, factorization and residue
  IMC: about −45 % tok/s.

## Critic responses

Every finding was applied unless stated below.

1. **"4.7 dB is not the DPS-48 tax; make DPS-48 the lead on an analog cell."** Accepted, with a
   correction. The shipped frozen cell *is* an INT4 slice cell (`sram6t_binary_caps`), so the F8
   mechanism applies literally there. That case is kept as `dps48_int4`, now at the F8
   **median** of 7.5 dB rather than the minimum. The analog-cell case is the new `dps48`,
   conditional on N3. The cell term is 0, not "<0.1 dB from mismatch": combos of W4 sources are
   exact integers in w + ax bits, and an exact ≥ 7 b write adds no error. The mismatch part stays
   at its full-scale-relative value because the per-tile digital scale normalizes each combo.
2. **"Pay-off = margin + ADC +1 b ≈ 1.7 dB; drop the 1/(r_t 2^{tax/3}) law."** Accepted for
   tok/s (12,903 at 1.0-1.5 dB). Disagreed that this is "nearly free": the same move costs
   **−62 % TOPS/W** (16.1 → 5.61), because the 27h1 converter term is ∝ 4^B and dominates pass
   energy. The doc now gives both thresholds. The old law is deleted. The cu grid now has 1.5 and
   3 fF.
3. **"DPS-48 dominates Strassen at every margin."** Upheld under both cell models: Strassen's
   algorithm tax alone (3.0 dB) exceeds the margin, and even tax-free it scores 12,527 against
   12,922. Its tax-free TOPS/W is slightly higher (16.3 vs 16.1, because there are fewer combo
   adds). The "Strassen wins between 3.0 and 4.7 dB" sentence is deleted. Orbit optimization is
   scored as the tax-free Strassen bound and as `dps48_strassen_orbit` (−3 dB, untested).
