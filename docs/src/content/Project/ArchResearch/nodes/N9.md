## Verdict in one paragraph

**Lead bundle (`lead`, the n9 DEFAULT):**

- Split supply: the analog rail stays at 0.7 V. Only the logic (rail, buffer, NoC) takes part in
  the 0.45 to 0.7 V sweep.
- RVT logic.
- **Row drive: 2 bits per plane switched between four rails** (0, V/3, 2V/3, V). That is 4 planes
  for 8 b instead of 6 bit-serial planes. The two intermediate rails come from two trimmed,
  tile-shared class-AB buffers.
- Full row swing (`v_exc_frac` 0.5, ±V_a/2). This is new, and swing is the largest circuit lever.
- **A bootstrapped NMOS column share/reset switch** (new). VGS is held at 0.7 V, so Ron is flat
  in the signal (measured).
- A class-A buffered reference (changed: the decap-only ratiometric reference is infeasible at 13 b).
- The N6 quiet comparator with a dynamic, capacitor-biased preamp.
- A DLL timebase locked to a replica column.

On the best base the other nodes allow (section 1), the lead scores **20,142 tok/s per die, 17.1
TOPS/W, 883 tok/W, 883 tok/J (projected, arch)** and **70,262 / 13.0 / 85.4 / 94.5 (sohu)**.

**Against the systolic baseline** (`baseline_systolic.py` with its ppa.json PE: 30,806 / 7.53 /
383 / 695 arch, 62,571 / 10.8 / 71.9 / 124 sohu):

- **arch:** 1.53x behind on the king metric. 2.3x ahead on TOPS/W, 2.3x on tok/W and 1.27x on tok/J.
- **sohu:** 1.12x ahead on tok/s and 1.20x on TOPS/W, but 1.32x behind on tok/J.
- With the same adaptive-clock margin on both sides (1.06), arch is 19,002 vs 30,249.

**The revision-1 honest negative was wrong and is withdrawn.** It said "circuit levers move
TOPS/W by a few percent". That holds for VDD and Vt flavours only. **Swing** decides how many
converter bits the gate needs, and the **column switch** sets the word time once the swing is
large, so both are first-order:

- Row swing from 0.25 to 0.5 turns an infeasible base into a feasible one at cu 1 fF.
- At full swing the ground-referenced NMOS switch sees VGS = 0.35 V at the signal extreme (5x its
  VS = 0 Ron, measured). It needs 32 fins to come within 4 % of the bootstrap at 8 fins.

**Converters are still 75 % of the tile pass energy** (81 of 108 fJ/MAC at 13 b), so the
remaining TOPS/W lever is whether the gate passes at 12 b (about 2x TOPS/W). That is set by N5,
N6 and N8 margins of 0.06 to 0.6 dB. Those margins are knife-edge: section 5.

## The model (equations, units, provenance)

`op()` is unchanged: e/delay/leak scale per flavour from 3.1. `evaluate_full` installs three hooks.

1. **`_arr_fix` on n2.array.** This sets the column switch, the settling and the word time.
   - Column excursion per side: `exc = k_sigma · V_a · v_exc_frac · rho(R) / sqrt(R)` (n5's
     column law). At the lead point that is 0.18 V.
   - `R_sw`, from the measured table at the worst signal point:

     | Switch | VGS at the worst point |
     |---|---|
     | ground NMOS (CM = exc) | V_a − 2·exc |
     | mid-rail NMOS | V_a/2 − exc |
     | bootstrap | 0.7 V |
     | TG | TG worst |

   - Settling is `(B+1)·ln2` time constants, not 8. With n2's `rc` timing:
     `slot = n_phase·(B+1)ln2·(R_sw/fins·C_col + τ_wire)·corner_margin + 4·FO4`.
   - Word time:

     | Driver | t_word |
     |---|---|
     | bit-serial | planes·slot |
     | k-bit DAC or rails | ceil(8/k)·slot, × 1.5 for k > 4 (row settle) |
     | PWM | 2.3·R_drv·C_row + 2^8·4·FO4_lib + slot (area-preserving RC) |

2. **`_acc_fix` on n5.accuracy.** These are new parts_db entries that the n8 gate sees.
   - `n9_droop`: hold-droop mismatch at 85 C, with
     `mean = fins·Ioff85·t_hold/C_tot`, `t_hold` = all conversion rounds,
     `Δ = √2·σ_rel·mean`, `σ_rel = sqrt(exp((σ_Vt/nU_t)²) − 1)`,
     `σ_Vt = A_vt/sqrt(fins·0.071·0.021 µm²)` (A_vt 1.3 mV·µm, projected) and nU_t = 1.2·kT/q at 85 C.
     The part is `S/((Δ² + (rej_cm·mean)²)/3)`.
   - Buffered DAC drivers add three parts: INL (rms from 3.3 against x_rms), C-DAC kT/C (the DAC
     is sized so this stays at or above 50 dB), and per-row gain spread
     `σ_A0/(β·A0^stages)` with σ_A0 10 % and β 0.5 (projected).
   - ml2 rails: rail error after trim of 0.1 % FS (projected), as level INL.
   - A swing above the driver's cap fails the gate.
3. **`plumb` on the tile.** Each term is labelled in the code.
   - DAC buffer energy is `E = C_row·V·(k+1)ln2/(β·gm/ID)` with β 0.5 and gm/ID 30 for the
     inverter pair (measured per device 18 to 22 at VGS 0.35, summed), × 2.5 for a compensated
     multistage. The row charge itself is n2's delivery and is not counted twice.
   - ml2 rails: the class-AB rail-buffer loss (η 0.5) on the half of the delivery that comes from
     the intermediate rails.
   - Bit-serial: the FO4 taper (⅓ of the load) plus one latch per row per plane.
   - Column-switch gate energy, which n2 does not price: `events·phys·mult·fins·Cgg·V²`, with
     mult 1 (NMOS), 2 (TG) or 3 (bootstrap). The bootstrap also adds area: a 5x boost cap and
     0.3 µm² of devices per column.
   - Digital periphery at liberty energy (5.4x n6's bare-device energy), plus 80 calibration
     gate toggles per conversion.
   - Reference options:

     | Reference | Model |
     |---|---|
     | ratiometric decap | `2^(B+1)·C_inj` per ADC |
     | reservoir | 32·C_inj per ADC, refill loss C_inj·V²/32 |
     | buffered | (1/η − 1)(delivery + C_inj·V²), η 0.3, plus static I_q and 4·C_inj of decap |
     | LDO | (1/η_LDO − 1) of the analog energy |

     C_inj is n6's DAC charge per conversion divided by V: 286 fF at the lead point.
   - Clock margin, only when `n9_margin_on`.

## 4. Per-option reasoning

### 4.1 VDD and supply

- **Split rail: lead.** At the peak the logic sits at 0.45 V and the analog at 0.7 V.
  Single-rail is −9 % TOPS/W at the same tok/s.
- **Converter energy rises at low VDD.** With the preamp (n6), `KAPPA_PRE·V/0.7 / LSB²` with
  LSB ∝ V goes as 1/V, so converter energy rises at low V. That is why the analog rail stays at
  the top of the range.
- **NTV everywhere costs 78 % of the king metric.** Its tok/J (1160) and TOPS/W (29.5) look good,
  but they belong to a different grid point (12 b, cu 2 fF) and come from the 12-b gate pass.
- **Per-phase DVFS stays merged.** Decode is compute-bound at the arch lead, and HBM-bound under
  sohu. The logic rail is already at the bottom of the sweep, and analog energy does not fall
  with the clock.
- **analog_rail_09** is reported only. The VDD range in ARCH_METRIC is written for the operating
  point. Whether it also binds a fixed analog rail is an open question for the user.

### 4.2 Device flavours

- **RVT logic: lead.** The rail is not binding.
- **SLVT logic costs 64 % TOPS/W** on today's rail model, worse than in revision 1. The rail
  leakage share has grown.
- **SLVT and LVT switches are out on hold droop at 85 C.** On today's 0.22 to 0.5 pF columns,
  8 SLVT fins give a mean droop of about 6.2 nA × 8 × 61 ns / 0.3 pF ≈ 10 mV, and the mismatch
  part fails the gate. RVT at 8 fins gives the `n9_droop` part of 51.5 dB (passes).

### 4.3 Column switch

- **Bootstrap: lead.** The column excursion at full swing is 0.18 V per side, because the
  k_sigma 16 clip-free range is wide. A ground-referenced NMOS then sees VGS = 0.35 V at the
  extreme, which is 5x its VS = 0 Ron, measured.
  - With the ground NMOS the word is 64.6 ns against the bootstrap's 52.3 ns, so it needs 32 fins
    to come within 4 %. With 32 fins it adds injection, which shows up as n5's 69.6 dB part.
  - The bootstrap keeps |VGS| = 0.7 V. Gate-to-bulk reaches 0.7 + VS ≈ 0.88 V. This is a
    reliability check, not a known failure.
  - The bootstrap costs 3x the gate charge and 431 µm² per tile (priced).
- **Mid-rail options are dominated.** The NMOS at V_a/2 − exc is 85 kΩ/fin (VGS 0.17 to 0.35 V).
  The RVT TG's worst case is 18 kΩ.
- **Falsifier:** a transient SPICE run of the bootstrapped switch whose charge injection or
  hold-mode feedthrough exceeds the 69.6 dB injection budget. Ground NMOS at 32 fins (−4 %) is
  then the fallback.

### 4.4 Amplifiers

- **Preamp: lead, named correctly now.** N6's KAPPA_PRE is anchored on Choo ISSCC 2021's
  capacitor-biased dynamic floating-inverter preamp, which draws no static current. That is the
  circuit N9 recommends.
  - The measured self-biased inverter A0 of 41 is *consistent with* that preamp having gain at
    0.7 V, but it does not establish KAPPA_PRE.
  - The 2.6x TOPS/W gap to amp_none is KAPPA_SA/KAPPA_PRE. Both constants are unmeasured at
    ASAP7, so it is projected until sim request 3 runs.
- **Closed-loop integrators: infeasible on the current gate.**
  - Single-stage ported integrator: 6-b cap.
  - Two-stage (A0 500, β 0.5): the loop gain limits ENOB ≤ log2(βA0) − 1 = 6.97 b.
  - Telescopic: headroom is fine. With measured gm/ID 18 to 22 at |VGS| 0.35, V_Dsat ≈ 0.1 V and
    five devices need about 0.5 V. Its A0 of about 200 caps a closed loop at 5.6 b.
  - The gate needs 12 to 13 b at k_sigma 16. Open loop, the telescopic is dominated by the
    inverter preamp (static current for no added noise benefit, 7a4).

### 4.5 References

- **The decap-only ratiometric reference is out.** To keep code-dependent bounce under ½ LSB at
  13 b it needs `2^(B+1)·C_inj` = 2^14 × 286 fF ≈ 4.7 nF per ADC, about 4 mm² per tile at
  20 fF/µm². Revision 1's "no energy, no area" was wrong.
- **Buffered: lead.** It costs 7.1 fJ/MAC (6.6 % of the pass) and 994 µm² per tile.
- **Reservoir** (32·C_inj per ADC; its deterministic droop is removed by SAR redundancy and
  foreground calibration, projected) gives +6 % TOPS/W and +5 % tok/J, but −11 % tok/s from its
  7,800 µm² of decap. Lexicographic order puts it second.
- **Bandgap + LDO** ties on tok/s, costs −4 % TOPS/W, and cannot be signed off in ASAP7.
- **Open:** supply droop between sampling and the bit trials (up to 61 ns) is not ratiometric
  for any of them (sim request 6).

### 4.7 Row drivers and input DACs

- **ml2 rails (2 b per plane, 4 rails): lead, narrowly.**
  - Against bit-serial (6 planes): +0.4 % tok/s (both are conversion-bound: t_conv 61.4 ns >
    t_word 52 ns), +9 % TOPS/W (4 planes of delivery instead of 6).
  - Cost: two trimmed rail buffers per tile and mid-rail steering in the cell (an N3 check: the
    V/3 and 2V/3 levels must pass the cell's steering switch, which needs a TG or the bootstrap).
  - **Bit-serial is a co-lead**: it is within model noise and needs no intermediate rails.
- **8-b row DAC: dominated (−80 %).** The measured INL of the 1-stage buffer caps the swing at
  0.275 (8 b at ½ LSB), and at that swing the gate needs cu 4 fF.
  - The 3-stage buffer reaches 0.43 but costs 2.5x the buffer energy: −41 %.
  - The nibble DAC (0.43 cap) passes only at 13 b with cu 2: −45 %.
- **Row DAC is no longer a tok/s lever.** With n2's RC slot the bit-serial word (52 ns) is
  already under the conversion time. Revision 1's x2.7 came from n2's 61-ns ported slot, which
  the live n2 no longer uses.
- **PWM: the corrected law makes it viable.** `t_word = 2.3RC + 256·73 ps + slot` (an
  area-preserving RC: the edge error is a code-independent pedestal) and `e = C_row·V²` once (the
  doubled factor of 2 is removed).
  - The result is 37 ns and +8 % tok/s, and it passes at 12 b: 32.4 TOPS/W.
  - **It is meaningless on a charge-domain cell**, because the bottom plate settles to the row
    level whatever the pulse width. It is listed as the best option *if* N2 adopts a
    current-integrating cell. Under sohu it falls to 27,824, because 12 b no longer passes there.
- **Source follower: infeasible** (0.25 V span).

## 6. Critic responses

Every finding was addressed. Where a finding was accepted, the fix is in the model.

1. **"Headline numbers do not reproduce; base infeasible."** Accepted. BASE now pins every node,
   it was re-derived on the live tree (n3 `gaincell_mom_caps_mom6`), and `--score` asserts
   feasibility. All revision-1 tables are void.
2. **"Row-DAC buffer: INL about 2 % FS, about 5 LSB; double standard against the OTA cap."**
   Partly refuted by measurement. The critic's arithmetic takes the *static gain error*
   1/(βA0) ≈ 5 % as nonlinearity. The measured closed-loop INL of the 1-stage buffer (3.3) is
   0.145 % FS peak at a 0.35 V span at SS. That is 0.37 LSB at 8 b, not 5 LSB, because A0 varies
   little over the span. The variation becomes INL only past about 0.45 V. There is no double
   standard: the integrator needs T ≥ 2^ENOB for a *gain* that sets the result, while the
   buffer's gain error is common to all codes and calibrated.
   Accepted: the energy law now divides by β = 0.5. The quoted current was wrong: it is about
   420 µA peak on today's 29 pF rows. INL, kT/C and gain-spread terms now reach the gate, and
   the INL table sets the swing cap. That cap is what kills the 8-b DAC, so the critic's
   conclusion holds for a different reason.
3. **"Row DAC ×2.7 depends on t_conv; bit-serial should be co-lead."** Accepted, and stronger
   than the critic said: on the live tree the row DAC loses (−80 %). Bit-serial is a co-lead.
4. **"PWM refuted: area-preserving RC; e double-counted."** Accepted. The law is corrected and
   PWM is marked viable only for a current-integrating N2 (4.7). The other critic's "upheld" PWM
   verdict assumed t_q ≥ 2.3RC, which is the error the first critic found.
5. **"SLVT droop fails ½ LSB; RVT droop understated."** Accepted. The droop-mismatch term at 85 C
   is in the gate, and SLVT switches are infeasible.
6. **"Ground NMOS Ron at VS = v_exc; settle_tau; injection."** Accepted, and it changed the lead
   switch. Ron is now from a measured VGS table at the worst point (V_a − 2·exc). Settling is
   (B+1)ln2. Injection was already in n5 (`k_inj`, ∝ share_fins), and that is why 32 fins lose
   12-b feasibility.
7. **"Mid-rail NMOS law invented."** Accepted. It is measured now (29.8 kΩ at VS 0.35). Still
   dominated on the current base.
8. **"Telescopic: V_Dsat misused."** Accepted: dominated, not infeasible, on headroom (4.4). The
   loop-gain cap of a closed loop still fails the gate.
9. **"Sync margin: digital 1.63, 'the same margin' not applied, TT is the convention."** Accepted.
   Scoring is at TT, and the symmetric margins are in the 4.6 table. The analog sign-off FO4
   ratio was measured at the liberty SS point: 1.425.
10. **"Analog energy is 1/V with the preamp" (upheld).** Stated in 4.1. The DVFS premise is
    re-checked: decode is compute-bound at the arch lead.
11. **"Preamp backs KAPPA_PRE" (upheld) / "A DC gain does not set a noise constant" (weakened).**
    The second is accepted. The lead circuit is renamed to Choo's dynamic floating-inverter
    preamp, the wording is "consistent with", and the 2.6x stays projected.
12. **"Ratiometric reference needs decap."** Accepted, with a correction. 2^(B+1) × C_dac at
    13 b and C_inj 286 fF is 4.7 nF per ADC. At 20 fF/µm² that is 230,000 µm² per ADC, not
    900 µm² per tile: the critic's estimate is about 1000x low. The decap-only reference is
    therefore infeasible, and the lead moved to buffered, with the reservoir as the alternative.
13. **"Missing multilevel drivers."** Added: `drv_nibble_dac`, `drv_ml3_dac` and `drv_ml2_rails`.
    ml2 rails became the lead driver. The 4-b nibble DAC loses on this base, because the base is
    conversion-bound and its 0.43 swing cap needs cu 2.
14. **"Droop and injection never reach the gate."** Accepted: `_acc_fix` puts them in parts_db.
15. **"Honest negative refuted: swing is the largest lever."** Accepted, with nuance. On the live
    tree n2 `charge_rail` already uses frac 0.5. N9 now caps swing per driver from measured INL.
    The lead runs at 0.5, and swing is what makes cu 1 fF feasible.
    Disagreement: swing is **not** set by the column-switch topology. The column node moves only

    The swing limit is the *row driver's* linear span (3.3). The column switch matters because it

16. **"Column switches tie; bootstrap judged on speed only."** Accepted. The bootstrap now leads
    for its measured signal-independent Ron.
17. **"Row DAC lead inconsistent; no DAC quality terms; doc timing wrong."** Accepted (items 2

18. **"Nibble DAC missing."** Added (item 13).

21. **"Base not the best; n3 not pinned."** Accepted (item 1).
