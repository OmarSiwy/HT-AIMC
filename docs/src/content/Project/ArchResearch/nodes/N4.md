## 1. Result

**Lead: `w8a8_lead`** (the module `DEFAULT`).
- **Weights:** INT8 per output channel, in two differential slices with magnitudes 3 + 4 bits. The LSB slice uses a
  0.25x unit capacitor, because its noise reaches the output attenuated 16x in amplitude.
- **Slice merge:** the two slice columns are charge-combined at 1:16 before **one** conversion per weight.
- **Rotation:** Hadamard, folded offline except at two inputs that run it online: down_proj (a 2048-point Kronecker
  block) and o_proj (cross-head, 32 points).
- **Activations:** A8 with one dynamic scale per (token, 32-row block). The block is at least the analog sum (R = 8
  live), so 27l4's Δe = 0 holds and the exponent is applied after the ADC.
- **Range:** k_sigma = 32 (N8's clip-free rule). n5 prices min(32, k_hard = sqrt(R)/rho = 22 σ), where nothing can clip.
- **Input encoding:** radix bit planes on N2's lead `hybrid_msb_digital`, with the top k_dig = 4 planes in the digital
  adder tree. **The amplitude DAC, my previous lead encoding, is out** (honest negative 3).
- **KV:** KV16 stays the default. KV8/KV4 are symmetric levers that the baseline also gets.

**Frames.** One option scores differently depending on the gate it is judged against. I report all four:
- **gate**: N8's gate for the format's tier as N8 ships it (`g43_lossless`: 43 dB + 1 dB die margin, clip 32 σ).
- **n2**: the same gate plus N2's hybrid credit of 6.02·k_dig dB. The digital MSB planes are exact, so the analog
  error reaches the output 2^k_dig smaller. That credit is N2's derived claim. N8 does not apply it yet, so `score`
  passes it as `q_credit_db`.
- **refit**: n2 plus a G2 target of 38.7 dB, my noise-plus-floor fit to the ADC sweep (law 3b). It is N8's call.
- **screen**: the falsified 28 dB gate. It only compares cost structures, with the ADC held at the format's law-3
  bits. **It is not a quality claim.**

### Honest negatives, stated loudly

1. **Without N2's MSB-digital credit, no analog W8 format competes.** Under N8's gate as shipped, the best W8 option is
   `w8a8_lead_q80` at 1,122 tok/s/die, and the lead reaches 119, against the baseline's 26,040. These points need
   SRAM caps at cu 16-32 fF and 13-14 b. They are compute- and floor-bound: column settling at large cu, n6 effective
   bits, and mismatch and thermal at 40-55 dB all bind together (finding 2). **Every competitive number in this doc
   depends on N2's 6.02·k_dig credit, which N8 has not adopted.**
2. **With that credit, the analog W8 lead is mostly a digital CIM.** Four of the six planes go through the digital
   adder tree. The analog path carries the low two planes with a 20 dB need. The format's rho lever is therefore small:
   Hadamard is worth +3.5 % TOPS/W (16.64 against 16.08 for `w8a8_lead_norot`). Per-token activations under rotation
   still reach 16.29. **Rotation and block scales are now quality levers more than SNR levers.**
3. **The amplitude DAC, my previous lead encoding, is out.** It has one plane, so N2's hybrid has nothing to send to
   the digital tree. With the live k_dig = 2 it gave negative analog energy (f_an = −1). On a pure-charge row it needs
   all 44 dB in analog: 176 tok/s/die (arch), 0 under Sohu. On MOS-gate caps its C(V) INL is unmodelled (27i1's
   caveat), so it is marked infeasible there until an ASAP7 transient exists.
4. **±16 σ is falsified (M).** With Hadamard and a per-tile ±16 σ range at 12 b, the proxy gives +1.67 % (T10). That is
   a clipping floor above G2's 1 %, as the reviewer's two-point fit predicted (+1.84 %). ±24 σ passes (T11, +0.45 %)
   and ±32 σ passes (T12, +0.21 %). Those runs are at harness R = 128. At the live R = 8, n5's k_hard bound
   (sqrt(R)/rho ≈ 22 σ) makes any k ≥ 22 clip-free by construction.
5. **My previous headline was wrong.** It claimed 28,267 tok/s and 34.0 TOPS/W on the screen, and "no format feasible
   under g43". The 0/0/0/0 had three causes: 16 σ failed N8's clip rule, the ADC grid stopped at 13 b, and the cu grid
   was inert on the gain cell. The 34.0 TOPS/W also had three: a 10-b screen ADC below the format's own law-3 bits, an
   amplitude DAC underpriced about 10x, and the n3 cell priced at 7 units per slice instead of the true 22 for W8.
6. **The stack moved during this revision.** Over the hour, n5 went from R = 32 to R = 8 and the n3, n6 and n8 defaults
   changed. The first re-score gave the lead 11.98 TOPS/W, and the final one gives 16.64, on unchanged N4 code. The
   ranking held across both snapshots: merge plus asymmetric caps beats plain, radix with the hybrid beats amp and
   bit-serial, and W4 > W5 > W8 > W6 one-cell. The absolute numbers belong to the joint search.

## 2. Equations (units; implemented in `n4_formats.py`)

| # | Term | Law | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HBM bytes/weight | `b_store / 8`; decode tok/s ≤ HBM_Bps / model bytes (Q8_0 = 8.5 b) | ARCH_METRIC streaming |
| 2 | Slices and units | `S = ceil((b_w − 1)/4)`, magnitudes MSB first (W8 = [3, 4], INT5 = [4]); a slice of m bits holds 2^m − 1 units/side. n3 prices 7 units/slice, so cap, area and array energy are rescaled (W8 x 22/14, INT5 x 15/7, one 8-b cell x 127/7); mismatch +10 log10(units ratio) | 27c4, formats.n_slices, n3 |
| 3 | ADC bits (statistical) | `B = ceil((SQNR_req − 10.79 + 20 log10 k)/6.02) + 1`, half-span FS = k σ, identical to n5's ADC part. Depends on SQNR and k, **not on the cell width** | D; R2/R3, T10-T12 |
| 3b | G2 SQNR (refit) | dPPL = a + b·10^(−SNR/10) through R2 (34.9 dB, +1.65) and R3 (46.9 dB, +0.61): a = 0.54 %, b = 3.4e3, so +1 % needs **38.7 dB** (N8's noise-injection law: 43.0) | D from M |
| 3' | ADC bits (faithful) | `ceil(log2(1 + N (2^bw − 1)(2^bx − 1)))` (+1 sign) | 27h6 (self-test) |
| 4 | Slice noise | y = 16 y_H + y_L with equal per-slice SNR: output SNR = slice SNR. An LSB drop of d bits changes it by `−10 log10((256 + 4^d g)/(256 + g))`, g = (σ_L/σ_H)² = 4.11² (M, R32): d = 1 −0.74 dB, d = 2 −2.9 dB, d = 4 −12.3 dB | D |
| 5 | Thermal | `(2 V_exc rho)² c_w / (2kT)`, V_exc = 0.35 V (n2 rail, VDD/2), so c_w = 0.425 fF/rho² at 44 dB (26 fF at rho 0.127); scored at 105 °C (−1.0 dB) | 27g2, n5 |
| 6 | Word time | radix: n2's planes × slot (top k_dig planes digital); bit-serial: planes × slot **and** (planes − k_dig) conversions; PWM 2^(bx−1) t_q; amplitude 1 slot; slice merge +1 slot | 27h5, 27h9 |
| 7 | Scale blocks | a weight or activation block g < R costs R/g conversions | 27l4 |
| 8 | Bit-serial vs radix | equal-bits bit-serial = B_x/3 × the radix thermal energy, B_x × switching and time; per-plane bits B − 1 (√3 larger LSB) | D, 27h1/27h5 |
| 9 | Online rotation | `(0.269 log2 2048 + 0.077 log2 32) R e_elem` per input block (down_proj, o_proj), plus the block max `R e_elem`, amortized over d/C output tiles | QuaRot, DD Priority Encoder |
| 10 | Amplitude DAC | per row: 2^7 × 0.2 fF C-DAC + **one analog buffer per row segment** (32 phys cols), `I = max((B+1) ln2 C_seg/(2/3 t_slot)/(gm/Id 15), 3 C_seg V_exc/t_slot)`, 25 µm² each | 27h9, review |

**Law 8 in brief.** The charge radix holds `v = 2^−B Σ 2^j v_j` and converts once, so its thermal energy is ∝ S²/(4σ_p²).
Converting every plane at equal error needs `Σ 4^(j−B) e² ≤ σ_p²/(3S²)`, so e = σ_p/S, and B planes cost
B S²/(12σ_p²), which is **B/3 × the radix**. Lagrange-optimal bits tie the radix. 27h5's 4^Bx/Bx gain assumes the
parallel converter resolves Bx more bits at the same LSB voltage, but the radix attenuates the sum by 2^−B instead.

## 5. Interactions

- **N2.** The lead depends on N2's hybrid and its 6.02·k_dig credit. N2 should guard `k_dig < input_planes` in
  `_hybrid`; `tile_effects` rejects that case today. Amplitude and PWM inputs need k_dig = 0 or a time cell.
- **N8.**
  - Adopt or refute N2's k_dig credit. It changes every analog number here by 100-1,000x.
  - T11 measures ±24 σ passing G2 at 12 b and T10 measures ±16 σ failing, so `lv_tile_range` should clip at 24, not 16.
  - Law 3b's refit puts G2 at 38.7 dB from the ADC sweep, against N8's 43.0 from noise injection.
  - The lossless gate hard-codes wbits ≥ 8. INT6 + Hadamard is lossless on the proxy (+2.4 %) but can pass only via
    `g43_w4_acceptable`, whose G2 target is identical. `score` routes wbits < 8 there.
  - N8's new default `bundle_lead` is not used by my frames (they pin the tier gate).
- **N5.** n5 should read `fmt["rho"]`. Its block law over-credits per-channel W8 by 6.6 dB, so `score` passes
  `rho_mode="fixed"` today. n5's new k_hard = sqrt(R)/rho is correct and makes N8's clip rule moot at R ≤ 32.
- **N3.** N3 prices 7 units per slice whatever the magnitude bits. The true W8 slice pair has 22 units (10.75 with
  the 0.25x LSB). I rescale in `tile_effects`; N3 should own that. My earlier report of negative N3 area did not
  reproduce, and I withdraw it.
- **N6.** Converter energy grows with the input range, so a larger rho costs energy once the SNR need is met. The
  1:16 slice merge is priced here; N6's `slice_merge` (a shared ADC) is a different mechanism.
- **N7.** The attention rail binds prefill. The 35 fJ/MAC rail buffer at R = 8 is now the second-largest energy term.
  The FWHT and block max add 0.6 fJ/MAC (law 9).
- **N1 and the baseline.** kv_bits stays 16 in DEFAULT, because `baseline_systolic` reads it from the n4 default.
  A metric knob read by both sides would remove the coupling (metric.py is not mine).

## 6. What a wrong choice here costs

- **Gating without N2's credit:** 119 tok/s/die (lead), and at best 1,122 (q80).
- **Amplitude DAC on the hybrid N2:** negative analog energy (a meaningless point), or 176 tok/s with k_dig = 0.
- **±16 σ at R128:** +1.67 % at 12 b (T10), a G2 fail at any width.
- **Two separate conversions per W8 weight, symmetric caps:** 10.43 against 16.64 TOPS/W (plain vs lead).
- **Per-token activations without rotation:** 1,639 tok/s (rho p10 0.013).
- **Q8_0 storage under HBM-bound decode:** −2.7 % tok/s against per-channel INT8.
- **W4 without the 8B certificate:** +7..9 % PPL (P). w4a8_lead's 1.21x tok/s over the W8 baseline is then a quality
  trade (27n1).
