# N5 round 2 (A3): accumulation and geometry

Owner: A3. Module: `scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n5_array.py` (new `r2_*` options, new keys and the
cost helpers `dequant`, `merge_cap_um2`; every round-1 option scores bit-identically, checked on all 39 options
in the core and joint frames). Designs: `designs/r2_A3_best.json` (ARCH), `r2_A3_sohu.json` (Sohu re-tune),
`r2_A3_cw.json` (contingent on the class weight), `r2_A3_corner.json` (corner-closed). Scoring frame:
`designs/r2_A3_frame.py`, so one command reproduces every number here. ESPice decks: scratchpad `a3/`.

Labels: **M** = measured (ESPice ASAP7 BSIM-CMG, this round), **D** = derived (a law applied to measured inputs),
**P** = projected. All four-metric scores are **P**, because they combine M, D and literature inputs.

**Revision 2 (critic round).** The first version of this page headlined 61,771 tok/s ARCH and 82,954 Sohu
("1.33x the Sohu-equivalent"). **That headline is withdrawn.** It was as-scored only (not judge-corrected), it
needed scratch patches, it left the dequant hardware and part of the ADC node unpriced, and its G2 margin
(+0.28 dB) existed only with the disputed +3 dB ADC class weight. Section 1 gives the corrected result and
section 9 answers each finding.

The ordered findings:

1. **LOUD: the round-1 pick's pooled conversion is physically inconsistent** (critic: upheld).
   `sar_direct_pool_k4` sums K = 4 R8 row tiles in charge before one conversion, but N5's block law assumes x
   and w scales per 8-row block.
   - With both scales shared over 32 rows, ρ(32)/ρ(8) = (m(8)/m(32))² = 0.577 (D), and the pick **fails G2 by
     −3.50 dB**.
   - The critic's better case: apply x per tile through each tile's own V/3, 2V/3 level rails. Then only w
     shares the 32-row block, and the pick still fails by **−1.54 dB**.
   - Pooling is valid only for formats whose w-scale block is ≥ K R.
2. **LOUD: costs the ranking frame never charged.** Each one is now in n5 and applied by `r2_A3_frame.py`:
   - E4M3 weight-scale bytes, +12.5 % of the weight stream at R8;
   - the dequant per conversion, now priced in area as well as energy: one 30 µm² unit (12 x 4-b multiply,
     exponent shift, 24-b accumulate, x-scale apply; P) per 2 ADCs is +1,920 µm² per tile (+5 %). One unit per
     ADC would be +3,840 µm² (+14 %);
   - the merge cap's MOM area (+3,200 µm² at cu 1.25);
   - the merge cap's load on the column node;
   - **new this revision:** the direct-SAR node also carries the packet-switch drains (2 per decision, 4 fins)
     and the residue-amp input pair (16 fins). The comparator gate is re-measured at its real bias (E3b,
     0.039 fF/fin, up from 0.024). At cu 1.25 the node and its parasitics are now 24.6 fF, which costs the old
     lead 0.12 dB.
3. **The bridge merge passes a linear-network topology check. It is not a verified block** (critic: weakened,
   accepted).
   - With ideal drive (E2, M) the bridge residual is 100–104 dB, the two-step merge 67–91 dB.
   - With real limits (E2b, M: 1 % MOM mismatch Monte Carlo, finite row drive, two-step switches upsized to
     32 fins, n5's m(8)) **both merges read 46–56 dB, and the two are statistically indistinguishable**:
     - bridge 45.6–54.6 dB, two-step 47.3–51.4 dB (TT, three seeds, plus SS and FF);
     - without mismatch, 52.8–56.3 dB.
   - The limit is the row drive, not the merge (E2c, M). Residual against the sample time after the row edge:

     | row drive | 0.10 ns | 0.20 ns | 0.30 ns | 0.45 ns | 0.65 ns | 0.85 ns |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | ideal, 1 Ω | 103 dB | 103 dB | 103 dB | 103 dB | 103 dB | 103 dB |
     | 8 fins, 744 Ω | 31 dB | 37 dB | 43 dB | 51 dB | 54 dB | 53 dB (floor) |
     | 32 fins, 186 Ω | 48 dB | 80 dB | 97–99 dB | 97–99 dB | 97–99 dB | 97–99 dB |

     n5's `row_gain` law (cal_res 0.05 trim + 20 log 1/e^(−t/τ)) predicts 32 / 43 / 52 dB at τ = 0.15 ns and
     0.1 / 0.3 / 0.45 ns, within 1 dB of the deck. It does not model the 8-fin ISI floor at 53–54 dB, which
     appears when the row has not returned to V_mid before the column reset releases.
   - **All A3 designs now use 32-fin row drivers** (`row_fins 32`). At R8 that is R × phys/row_seg × 32 fins,
     under 1 % of the tile, and it does not change the score.
   - The one-event slot removal stands on the topology, since the bridge needs no merge phase. The frame applies
     it. Landing it in n4 needs n4's owner.
4. **The speed verdict is restated** (critic: weakened, accepted). Faster accumulation pays only when the word
   binds the pass, and **N9's buffer bias was not what blocked it**:
   - At cu 1.25 the pass is conversion-bound and ARCH concurrency is KV-capacity-bound (B = 551), so a faster
     share switch does nothing.
   - At cu 1.75 (the new lead) the larger column slows the share, the word binds, and the share switch pays:
     16 → 24 → 32 → 48 fins gives 39.3k → 44.2k → 47.1k → 50.4k as scored (+28 %). Corrected it gives 39.3k →
     44.2k → 46.9k → 46.7k, because the x1.25 ADC time makes conversion bind again at 6.42 ns. 32 fins is chosen
     for its thicker margin.
   - When n9's ml2 bias is scaled by the run clock and word duty (`--bias duty`, the critic's patch), ARCH tok/s
     does not change (KV-bound) and tok/J rises 3–4 %. FAST S2 under Sohu goes from 48.2k to 68.1k as scored, so
     the old "speed is power-negative" verdict came from n9 charging the bias always-on.
   - More converters (S2, S1) still lose on area (+45 %, +133 %), and S8 is conversion-bound (10.3 ns).
5. **Geometry verdicts stand** (critic: upheld; re-run at the new lead in the A3 frame, with dequant priced):
   - R8 is the only feasible row count. R16 fails at −0.57 dB, and R4 fails on its row drive (−43 dB: the
     4-row column at cu 1.75 sees ε_row > 10 %).
   - C128 and C512 tie with C256 (44,180 / 44,203 against 44,203).
   - Single-ended fails at −3.74 dB (−1.45 even at cu 3.5), pseudo-differential at −2.81 dB.
   - Current summation is excluded by the n4 merge guard.
   - The two-step merge scores 38,853 against 44,203 for the bridge at sf24 (the merge slot), so the bridge
     still wins on time.

## 2. Equations (n5 additions, units)

- **Merge load**, W8 split 3 + 4 magnitude bits, LSB unit `cu_lsb` (n4 `f_cu_lsb`):
  - `C_L = C_col · 15/7 · cu_lsb` (fF), and `s = 1/(16 cu_lsb)` is the LSB charge fraction the 1:16 weight needs.
  - Two-step: load `C_x = s C_L/(1−s)`. Bridge: `C_b = s C_L/(1−s)` in series, so its load on the MSB node
    is `C_b C_L/(C_b + C_L) = s C_L`.
  - At C_col 56 fF and cu_lsb 0.25: C_L = 30 fF; load 10 fF (two-step) or 7.5 fF (bridge). It adds to `C_par` (D).
- **Direct-SAR node**, per column (critic revision):
  `c_in = (share_fins + 8) C_off + sel C_on + [S K sel C_off + cmp C_gate + 2 B pkt C_off + ra C_gate + (K R + 4 S) pitch c_w]/K`.
  - The packet term counts only when n6 `adc = sar_direct` and `dac != bp`. The residue-amp term counts only
    when `fine = ra`. A sampled or bottom-plate CDAC is priced by n6 itself, as attenuation.
  - Per-fin caps, ×2 for MOL/contact (P):
    - C_off = 0.0113 and C_on = 0.0263 fF/fin (E3, M);
    - C_gate = 0.0390 fF/fin (E3b, M), at VGS 0.35 V with VS = 0, triode at the end of evaluation. It is 0.0349
      saturated and 0.0598 at VGS 0.7.
  - Defaults: pkt_fins 4, ra_fins 16, cmp_fins 16.
- **Pooled scale**, `pool_scale = "shared"`: block = K R rows, `ρ = √(E[u_x²] E[u_w²]) / m(K R)²` (D), scale
  bytes `8/(K R wbits)`.
- **Dequant** (`n5.dequant`, block mode):
  - conversions per pass `C · (1 if merge else slices)/K`;
  - energy `dq_e_mac` (2) rail INT8-MAC equivalents each (P);
  - area `ceil(n_adc/dq_share) · dq_um2` (30 µm², P: ASAP7 FA ≈ 0.2 µm², DFF ≈ 0.3 µm²). dq_share 2 needs one
    12 x 4 multiply-add per half conversion (≥ 0.7 ns at the lead, about 20 FO4 at 0.45 V, P).
- **Merge-cap area** (`n5.merge_cap_um2`): `2 C · s C_L/(1−s) / ρ_MOM`, with ρ_MOM = 2 fF/µm² (repo constant;
  n3's dense MOM6 is 4.78, so this is conservative).
- **A3 frame** (`designs/r2_A3_frame.py`): the joint frame plus stored weights × (1 + `scale_bytes_frac`),
  dequant energy and area, merge-cap area, and the bridge slot removal. Flags: `--corrected`, `--no-cw`,
  `--bias duty`.
- **Settling law check** (E1): one share between C_col and an equal hold cap settles to 2⁻¹³ in ≈ 8.9 τ. At 16
  fins τ = 14.7 ps (M), so t13 = 130 ps measured against the n2 + n9 law's 304 ps (2.3x conservative). The law
  turns optimistic at 64 fins (65 ps measured against 32 ps).

## 5. Delay vs area vs power (the user's trade)

- **Minimal delay**: the cheap lever is the share switch. 32 fins over 24 is +6.6 % ARCH as scored (+6.0 %
  corrected) for +0.3 % area. Wider row drivers cost nothing in score and close the E2c floor. More converters
  (S2, S1) cut no time on a word-bound tile and cost 45–133 % in area. FAST pays only with N9 duty-gated
  buffers.
- **Minimal area**: C128 halves the tile at equal density. S8 saves 22 % of the area but loses 6–19 % of tok/s
  to conversion time. cu is the dominant area knob: each +0.25 fF costs about 4.8k µm² per tile and 13–18 % of
  tok/s.
- **Power**: at ARCH the lead is KV-capacity-bound (B = 551, VDD 0.45), not power-capped. At Sohu, and under the
  corrected rule, the power cap binds and the operating-point search picks half-clock points (section 7).
- **Transistors per tile**: the bridge merge removes 2 bootstrapped switches per side per column, about 6.7k
  devices per tile (D), and adds 512 MOM caps. The dequant units add about 64 × 30 µm² of logic (dq_share 2).
- **The quality gate sets the tile.** At the pessimistic G2 rule, cu 1.75 is the smallest unit cap that
  passes. It costs −22 % against the cu 1.25 tile that only passes with the class weight.

## 7. What this does to other blocks

- **N4**: drop the +1 merge slot when n5 `merge_load == "bridge"`. The frame does this today (+10 % ARCH at the
  slot-bound points). Adopt n5's merge-cap area.
- **N6**: `cascade_K > 1` needs `pool_scale = "shared"`. Under it pooling fails on R8 block formats by −3.5 dB
  with shared x, or −1.5 dB with per-tile x through the rails. n6 `_c_col_fF` should read n5's `c_par_fF`.
- **Core / model.py** (integrator):
  - move `n5.scale_bytes_frac`, `n5.dequant` and `n5.merge_cap_um2` into `model.tile` / the workload. Until then
    `designs/r2_A3_frame.py` applies them;
  - make the operating-point search monotone: a faster tile must never score below a slower one, since it can
    run at the slower point. The corrected Sohu rows marked * show the defect (12.8 ns chosen where 6.4 ns
    exists);
  - re-rank the finalists in the A3 frame with `--corrected`.
- **N9**: scale the ml2 buffer bias with the run slot and the word duty. It decides FAST (+20 % corrected ARCH
  for FAST S2 with duty). Time the row return so that the row is back at V_mid before the column reset releases
  (the E2c 8-fin floor). Use ≥ 32-fin row drivers at R8.
- **N3**: cu 1.75 fF units (+75 % MOM area per weight over 1 fF). The corner closure depends on the MOM mismatch
  band, which is still unmeasured.
- **N8**: run the SmolLM2 harness at 38.8 and 39.2 dB with and without the class weight. It decides between
  r2_A3_cw (+16 % ARCH corrected) and r2_A3_best.
- **N2**: E1 measures τ = 14.65 ps (56/56 fF, 16 fins), matching A2's R_COL_FIN.
