# N5: Array geometry and analog accuracy

2026-10-05, revision 2 (after two adversarial critics). Owner module:
`scripts/compiler/metrics/arch_eval/nodes/n5_array.py`. Objective:
[ARCH_METRIC.md](../../ARCH_METRIC.md): tok/s per 100 mm² die (king) > TOPS/W > tok/W > tok/J.

**Labels.** Every system number here is **projected**: laws applied to measured ASAP7 devices
(n3's MOS C(V), switch Ron, comparators), measured sky130 anchors and literature constants. One
input is **measured here**: the operand statistic ρ on the repo's SmolLM2 tensors (§2). The
noise, range, settling and scale-format laws are **derived** (law on measured parameters). The
coupling, injection residual, reference droop and common-mode rejection coefficients are
**projected** and are listed as simulation requests. The systolic baseline reads
`digital/sysreference/build/ppa.json` (synthesized ASAP7 PE: area and fmax measured, energy
derived; see that file's notes), so its numbers are **derived**, not literature-projected.

**What changed in revision 2.** (1) The converter range is hard-bounded: |Σxw| ≤ R after block
scaling, so the range is min(k_σ, √R/ρ) = 9.4σ at R8, not 16σ. (2) N4's format is now a swept
axis (`w8a8_lead_amp`, N4's snapshot DEFAULT, and `w8a8_2slice`), and every design is scored
through N4's `tile_effects` (C-DAC area and energy, per-slice conversions). (3) The core
corrections N5 asks for are applied and labelled: scale bytes on weight bytes only, a CPP-wide
dummy edge per strip (full-cell ring as the worst case), one conversion per differential pair,
and a priced dequant multiply. (4) Scales are exact for x (computed on die) and E4M3 for w, with
the ρ loss charged. (5) The comparison is iso-precision: the W8/KV8 systolic baseline. (6) Sohu is
scored on both the evaluator's calibrated die and ARCH_METRIC's 666 mm², with N8's 70B penalty.
(7) Eleven options were added. The **verdict changed**: the earlier lead "ties the baseline" is
replaced by a projected win on tok/s, TOPS/W and tok/W at the bundle gate, a loss on tok/J, a loss
under g43, and a loss under Sohu once the 70B penalty applies (§5).

**Context.** Scores use the **frozen snapshot of the nodes taken 2026-10-05 21:54 EDT** (scratchpad
`n5/snap`, plus this revised n5). Other nodes have kept changing since (live 23:xx: n3 default
`gaincell_mom_caps_mom6`, n4 `w8a8_lead`, n6 `sar_direct_residue_amp`, n8 default `bundle_lead`).

## 1. The law that decides this node

A passive charge column at ASAP7 averages R products onto C_col = R·C_w (27g2, figure):

```
v_sig   = g a V_exc rho(R) / sqrt(R)                 [V]   g = 2 differential, 1 single-ended
noise   = n f_acc kT / C_tot,  C_tot = C_col / a     [V^2] n = 2 diff, 1 SE, 1 + 1/m_ref pseudo
SNR_th  = (g^2/n) a V_exc^2 rho(R)^2 C_w / (f_acc kT)      -> independent of R (27g1, 27g2)
```

(Revision 1 wrote a² here; the code has a, because the noise sits on C_tot = C_col/a.) Rows do
not enter SNR_th: each unit cap brings kT/C of charge noise with its own signal charge (27g2,
"energy floor 12kT·4^B independent of supply"). R enters through ρ(R) (§2), the converters (1/R
conversions per MAC, 27h1, 27n5), the hard range bound √R/ρ, and settling and write time.

**V_exc.** The scored designs use n2's `v_exc_frac` 0.5, i.e. V_exc = VDD/2 = 0.35 V. Revision 1
said VDD/4 and listed rail-to-rail drive as an unclaimed +6 dB. That was wrong: the +6 dB is
already in every score. For MOS units the swing is set by n3's inversion window [0.45, 0.70] V
(mos_k = (0.25/0.35)² = 0.51, folded into C_eq), so the remaining lever is n3's window (e.g.
`gaincell_mos_r08_vlo035`), and widening it costs odd-order INL (§3: 58 dB → 47 dB) plus TDDB/BTI
risk.

**What sets the capacitor.** At the lead, thermal is 43.9 dB and mismatch is 42.9 dB, and N8
charges static mismatch 3.6 dB more (39.3 dB effective). So the **A_VT mismatch law** (n3's MOS
unit σ) plus N8's static penalty sets the capacitor, and kT/C is the floor. This agrees with
AC27gi/27g2 ("unit cap sized by mismatch/injection, not kT/C"). The worked thermal number for
R32, ρ 0.18, V_exc 0.35 V, 38.5 dB is C_w ≈ 3.7 fF per weight-slice at a = 1 (5.3 fF at a = 0.7);
revision 1 printed 14 fF at 0.175 V.

## 2. The operand statistic ρ (measured here) and the scale format

ρ = rms(Σ_j x_j w_j) / (√R · x_fs · w_fs) over R-row blocks of the repo's saved SmolLM2 operands
(`scripts/compiler/out/{acts,programming}`, 7 projections, 9 tokens):

| Operands | ρ (median, range) | Label |
|---|---|---|
| As quantized (per-tensor INT8 x, per-channel INT4 w) | 0.037 (0.017-0.040) | measured |
| Random rotation of K + per-token INT8 / per-64-group INT4 | 0.12-0.17 | measured |
| Rotated, Llama-3-8B widths | 0.10 | projected (order statistic) |
| x and w scales per R-row block, R = 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 | 0.32 / 0.24 / 0.175 / 0.13 (no rotation); 0.32 / 0.24 / 0.19 / 0.16 (rotated) | measured |
| Same, law ρ(R) = 1/E[max\|z\|]² | 0.314 / 0.231 / 0.182 / 0.148 | derived (within 10 %) |

**Scale format (new, derived).** A scale rounded up to a coarse grid leaves the block max at
u ≤ 1 of full scale, which multiplies ρ by √E[u²] per operand:

| Scale format | E[u²] | ρ factor | dB per operand | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact (x, computed on die per token) | 1 | 1 | 0 | none in HBM |
| E4M3 (w, 1 byte per block) | 0.915 | 0.957 | −0.38 | a real dequant multiply on the rail (charged: 200 fJ per logical output per pass, derived from ppa.json 252 fJ/INT8 MAC × 14/8 × 0.45) |
| E8M0 (power of two) | 0.541 = 0.75/ln 4 | 0.736 | −2.67 | dequant is a shift |

The lead uses exact x and E4M3 w: ρ(8) = 0.301. Revision 1 combined "shift dequant" (E8M0) with
the exact-scale ρ. Both critics caught this, and it is fixed. **Decoupled blocks:** the x block must equal R
(the column sum takes one x scale), but the w block may be longer: ρ = 1/(m(R)·m(w_block)) and the
HBM overhead is 8/(w_block·w_bits) (option `wblk32`, §4c).

**Range (new, derived).** After scaling, |x|, |w| ≤ 1, so |Σxw| ≤ R. The column cannot swing past
k_hard = √R/ρ σ: 9.4σ at R8, 18σ at R16, 33σ at R32 (E4M3 w scale). N8's 16σ (bundle) and 32σ (g43) clip
requirements were measured on per-tensor R128 partials and cannot bind below k_hard, where nothing
clips. n5 prices min(k_σ, k_hard) and drops the clipping term when the bound applies. n8's gate
still sees the requested k_σ, so no n8 change is needed. This cuts the lead's converter from 14 b
to 12 b nominal and is the largest single change in this revision.

## 3. Error budget (module docstring has every equation)

Lead values: bundle gate, R8 C256 S4, `w8a8_lead_amp`, gain-cell MOS 32 fins, 12-b ADC, VDD 0.7 V.

| Term | Law | Source | Lead (dB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| thermal | (g²/n) a V_exc² ρ² C_w/(f_acc kT) | 27g2, 6b1, 18q | 43.9 |
| mismatch (static) | 1/σ_u², σ_u from n3 (MOS: A_VT·ΔC/q, A_C projected) | 27i1, 27d6, n3 | **42.9** (binding; n8 −3.6 dB) |
| column settling | e^{2t/τ}/2, open line τ = r c L²/π² | derived; 27f9, 15p1 | ≫ 100 |
| row RC (per-column gain) | ε = e^{−t/τ_row}, trimmed to 5 % | 27d7, 21i1, 15j | 109 |
| column-column coupling | 1/(2ε_cc²), ε_cc 0.5 % shielded, ×0.1 diff twist | 15o1/15o2, 3a1 | 63 |
| input-line coupling | (ρ/(ε_rc (VDD/V_exc) x_rms))², ×rej_cm | 20w6a | 75 |
| share-switch injection | k_inj ½ C_gg VDD / C_tot | 27g2, 18o1a, 18p1 | 79 |
| MOS C(V) even order | δ = 2.05 % (n3 measured), ×rej_cm (diff only) | 18c1, 6x; n3 | 62 (diff) / 36 (SE) |
| **MOS C(V) odd order (new)** | cubic part of Q(V) = ∫C dV under amplitude drive; the pair does not cancel it | derived from n3's C(V) | 58 (47 at a [0.35, 0.8] V window) |
| reference droop | 1/ε_ref², ε_ref 0.2 % | 24n5 | 54 |
| ADC | 12·4^B/(2k)², k = min(k_σ, k_hard) = 9.4 | 27h6, 21g2 | 45.0 |
| clipping | none (hard-bounded range) | §2 | - |
| **total** | | | **38.8** (target 37 + margin: N8 margin +0.03 dB) |

The odd-order term is new. N4's amplitude C-DAC drives each MOS unit to one of 256 levels, so the
INL of the gate capacitor's Q(V) enters directly. Binary drive has no INL (two levels), and the
pair cancels the even part. At n3's default window the term is 58 dB, which does not bind. At
n3's wide window (`vlo035`, rail 0.8 V) it is 47 dB, which binds under g43.

## 5. The lead, its corrections, and where it wins and loses

**Lead: differential column pairs, R = 8 rows per conversion, C = 256 outputs, 4 columns per ADC,
no checksum, x scale exact per 8 rows (on die) and w scale E4M3 per 8 rows, range 9.4σ (hard
bound), shielded/caged columns, per-column trim; with N4's `w8a8_lead_amp` (one amplitude slot),
n2 `charge_rail`, n3 gain-cell + 32-fin SLVT MOS units, 12-b ADC.** SNR 38.8 dB, N8 margin
+0.03 dB. Prefill is bound by the rail's attention rate, decode by HBM, and concurrency by KV
capacity. The array is no longer the bottleneck.

| Score (bundle, arch) | tok/s/die | TOPS/W | tok/W | tok/J |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw (core as is, with N4 tile effects) | 44,221 | 16.4 | 768 | 768 |
| **corrected (scale bytes on w only, CPP edge 3.0 %, one conversion per pair, dequant 51 pJ/pass)** | **49,135** | **13.6** | **658** | **658** |
| corrected with the full-cell ring (25 %, worst case) | 49,135 | 13.6 | 658 | 658 |
| W8/KV8 systolic baseline | 41,356 | 7.7 | 424 | 839 |
| **lead / baseline** | **+19 %** | **1.77x** | **1.55x** | **−22 %** |

Each correction moves the score in a known direction. Scale bytes cost the weight stream 12.5 %.
One conversion per pair halves t_conv, which is the largest gain. The dequant multiply is 18 %
of the pass energy (51 of 285 pJ) and is why corrected TOPS/W is below raw. The ring does not move
tok/s at the ceiling. Revision 1's correction applied the scale overhead to the whole HBM
bandwidth, KV included (critic: −3.3 %, not −5.6 %). That is fixed. The pass energy is 285 pJ:
converters 151 (53 %), buffer 72, dequant 51, recombination 7, array 3.

**Where it loses or is fragile (all projected):**

penalty applies (−17 %).** The win needs all of the following. (a) N8's 7-dB bundle credit holds
on block-scaled operands. The lead has 0.03 dB of margin, so it rests on the credit entirely
(§5a). (b) N6's converter energy is within ~3x of its model. (c) The core adopts the
one-conversion-per-pair contract. Without it the raw score is +7 %. Confidence: **low**.

**Robust alternative.** `blk_diff_r16_c256_s4` with MOM 1 fF units scores 41,883 / 23.1 / 956 /
1008 (+1 % tok/s, 3.0x TOPS/W, 2.3x tok/W, +20 % tok/J). It beats the iso-precision baseline on all
four metrics, uses linear MOM caps (no C(V) risk, no MOS mismatch extrapolation), has 0.6 dB of
margin, and survives converter energy x4 at 38.3k. It loses lexicographically to the R8 lead by
15 % on tok/s. Recommendation: keep R8 as DEFAULT and carry R16/MOM as the fallback until N6 and
N8 measure.

**Converter check (critic 2).** At the lead, the hard-bounded range brings the converter down to
12 b nominal (≈ 9.9 effective). With one conversion per pair it does one differential conversion
per ~0.95 ns at ~295 fJ: P ≈ 0.31 mW at f_s ≈ 1.05 GS/s, SNDR ≈ 61.5 dB, so the Schreier FoM is
≈ 154 dB (derived). That is ~15 dB inside the published envelope at ~1 GS/s (projected,
Murmann survey). Revision 1's 14-b point sat at FoM ≈ 188.5 dB, near the thermodynamic bound, as
the critic computed. The ask to N6 is therefore **12 b nominal in ~1 ns per differential
conversion**, not 14 b. The x4/x10 rows above give the exposure that remains.

two very likely overlap (27n3: SpinQuant made the model less noise-robust). With the credit cut to
the non-Hadamard levers (2 dB, target 42 dB), the full 120-point joint search gives (projected):

## 8. Open questions (core / other nodes)

1. model.py has no topology key. It converts a pair twice (t_conv doubled; corrected here by
   halving it). A `conv_per_phys` key in the geometry contract is needed. Without it the lead
   scores 44.2k raw instead of 49.1k.
2. model.py has no area hook for the dummy edge and does not charge per-block scale bytes. N5
   applies both as corrections. A `scale_bits_per_weight` format property (N4) and an
   `edge_um` tile term are the minimal hooks.
3. One settling owner (n2's slot vs n5's open-line term, ~5x apart). The slot now sets the R
   optimum, so this is the most important unresolved law in N5.
4. n2 `hybrid_msb_digital` × 1-plane encodings gives negative energy (§6 N2). Live n2 still has it.
5. `test_arch_eval.py` "default design: four finite positive metrics" fails on the live nodes.
   The live default (n3 `gaincell_mom_caps_mom6` at its default cu, n6 8 b) misses N8's target by
   0.1-0.2 dB at 12-14 b, and at the n6 default 8 b the ADC term (26.5 dB) fails outright. N5 now
   requests k_σ = 32 by default, which satisfies every n8 clip check at no cost below k_hard
   (before this change the live default also failed `clip_ok`). The remaining gap is the joint
   default cap/bits.
6. The quality harness was not used (deferred). Every gate is N8's projected/derived target.

## 9. Critic responses

Where a critic was right the fix is in the model and the numbers above. Disagreements and partial
agreements:

1. **"R128 C256 S1-2 with `w8a8_lead_amp` scores 49.7k and beats the N5 lead by 1.7x; r64/r128
   should be re-verdicted viable" — refuted.** I reproduced the 49,841 with `model.evaluate`. It
   rests on two artifacts. (a) n2's DEFAULT `hybrid_msb_digital` subtracts k_dig = 2 digital
   planes from the 1 amplitude plane: f_an = −1, so the analog energy enters negative and the word
   time collapses to the adder-tree floor of 1.98 ns. n2's own slot for that column is 335.7 ns
   (`diag.py`: same design, `t_word 1.98` via model.py vs `slot 335.65`). (b) `model.evaluate`
   skips N4's `tile_effects`, so the C-DAC area and energy (314 pJ/pass at R128) and the
   slot-based word time are never applied. Scored coherently (n2 `charge_rail`, N4 effects on),
   R128 C256 S2 is 1,467 and the best R128 is 1,870. The **format axis itself was a valid
   omission**: it is now swept, and the amplitude DAC becomes the lead's format (+33 % at R8).
   "Short columns win" holds under both encodings, because the deciding term is the slot on C_col,
   not the plane count.
2. **"Missing: per-slice heterogeneous accuracy, +20-30 % tok/s" — partly upheld.** The 1/256
   argument holds for block-normalized W8 (LSB slice ≈ 0.4 % of output power at R8, 1.1 % at
   R128; derived from the slice statistics). It does not hold for per-channel W8, which is why
   N4's 4-b drop was falsified (T5, measured). Scored as `lsbthin` (LSB caps /15, ADC −2 b,
   ≤ 0.5 dB total): tok/s ties the lead at the ceiling (49,135), TOPS/W +9 %, tok/J +35 %. That is
   the lexicographic co-lead, not +20-30 % tok/s, because the lead is ceiling-bound. It stays
   off DEFAULT until N8's harness measures LSB-slice noise on block-scaled weights.
3. **"MOM/12-b is a near-tie, prefer it" — superseded.** Under the hard-bounded range the MOS
   point no longer needs 14 b, and both points are 12 b. At R8 the lead's MOS32 wins on tok/s, but the
   power-cap fragility the critic found is real: at x4 converter energy R8 falls to 38.8k while
   R16/MOM holds 38.3k. R16/MOM is now listed as the robust alternative (§5).
4. **E4M3 "~0.3 dB loss" — corrected to 0.38 dB per operand** (E[u²] = 0.915, computed). The rail
   multiply is priced at 200 fJ per logical output per pass. That is not negligible (18 % of pass
   energy at R8), which is why E8M0 recovers +10 % TOPS/W for −6 % tok/s.
5. **Scale decoupling (`wblk32`) — scored, dominated.** ρ drops 2.5 dB, the required capacitor
   grows (MOS64), and the tile becomes compute-bound at 47,446. The HBM saving (9.4 % of the weight
   bytes) does not pay back at R8.
6. **Exact low-resolution conversion — scored, dominated (136 tok/s).** For σ_n ≤ LSB/8 on a
   360-level partial it needs 48.7 dB per conversion (more than g43's 44 dB), and 4x the
   conversions. "Zero gate risk" costs 360x. It is on record as the critic asked.
7. **Bit-serial f_acc — upheld.** The recursion is binary weighting with no extra halving, and
   the noise settles at ~(2/3) kT/C plus the per-plane sampling. f_acc 1.5 makes it tie the
   word-level share on tok/s (22,361 at R32), with tok/J 5 % lower. Re-verdicted viable. The
   transient-noise sim stays a request.
8. **Ring = 2 CPP + break, ~2.4 % — upheld, computed 3.0 %** at the R8 pitch (1.34 µm). The full-cell
   ring is reported as the worst case. It does not change tok/s at the ceiling.
9. **Sohu die 666 vs 1,063 mm² — both scored.** `metric.sohu_area()` (the evaluator's calibration
   against the current ppa.json) returns 1,063 mm². ARCH_METRIC's text says 666 mm² from a
   different calibration. That discrepancy is the core owner's to resolve. The relative verdict
   is the same on both dies.
10. **"Upheld" findings** (thermal R-independence, SE/pseudo, ρ(R) law, streaming kills for tall R):
    kept. The a² typo is fixed (§1).
