## Loud corrections (round 2b, after the critic)

1. **The first A2 lead (`charge_rail_bps2`, rf 96, sf 16, 67,829 / 14.79) is infeasible.** Its row level IS the
   multiply reference, and it comes from the local VDD/GND grid. One row switching draws c_row V from the tile
   decap, so the level droops by an amount set by the plane's input popcount. That error is common to every
   column, does not cancel differentially, and cannot be trimmed after the analog plane merge. Priced (below),
   it is a 34-dB term (1.53 % droop per row with the whole array footprint as MOS decap, an upper bound). G2
   falls to −7.33 dB. **Every grid-driven row has this term, including the round-1 pick's V level and the n9
   lumped bit-serial option (O4); none of them priced it.**
2. **The bps2 slot was 11-31 % too short.** Two cascaded poles settle slower than the slower pole alone,
   the real bootstrap (boost cap, VGS about 0.58 V) is weaker than the ideal VCVS, and the crosspoint TG adds a
   floor on the column loop. Measured (sp4, sp6): the top plate reaches 2⁻¹³ in 437 ps at rf 96 / sf 16, against
   the old law's 334 ps. The new law (fitted, never faster than the measurement) gives slot 1.36 ns for the old
   sizing: word 9.53 ns, multiply-bound.
3. **Bit-serial delivery was 8× underpriced.** It was the sky130 anchor law at v_exc = 0.35 V: 0.68 fJ per
   crosspoint per word. The inverter steps the connected plates 0 → V with the top held, so it costs
   p1 · C_on · V² per plane: 2.7 fJ, or 5.4 fJ with the constant-charge dummy. n9's buffered reference then
   adds its (1/η − 1) loss on top. The lead's pass energy is 413 pJ, against 270 pJ in the first report.
   **TOPS/W falls from 14.8 to 9.8 (live), and the ARCH point becomes power-capped.**
4. **Driver area and fins were counted on logical columns.** There are 1,024 physical columns per row
   (256 × 2 slices × 2 differential). Driver area is now per physical column; the counts below are recounted.
5. **The ranking frame.** The first report used only the as-scored 12-b SAR. Its "+50 % over the honest pick"
   is now +36 % (live) and +15 % (judge), and the judge frame is the one that ranks.

Round-2 corrections 1-5 of the first report still stand: the pick's lumped slot has no row phase, the 4-level
rail cannot run at 1.13 ns, the column-switch event count, analog weights are destroyed under streaming reuse,
and row drivers had been unpriced.

## Equations (n2_domain `_charge_meas`, units ns, fF, Ω)

Per input plane, with τ_c the column, τ_r the row segment and τ_w the column wire:

    τ_c  = KB_BOOT · R_COL_FIN / share_fins · C_col + TAU_COL0 + τ_w     R_COL_FIN 6,886 Ω·fin (E1), KB_BOOT 1.1, TAU_COL0 13 ps (fit)
    τ_r  = (R_drv_fin / row_fins + R_strap/2) · C_seg                   R_drv_fin 8,100 (inverter, refit sp4) | 8,900 (rail TG)
    C_seg = row_seg · (c_w + pitch · c_wire);  R_strap = n5 law ∝ row_seg
    casc(τ_r, τ_c, ε): first t with |τ_c/(τ_r − τ_c) (e^(−t/τ_r) − e^(−t/τ_c))| < ε,  ε = 2^−(B+1)
    bps2: slot = m · 2 · max(casc, n_r τ_r, n_c τ_c, rail floor) + 4 FO4    (m = 1.2; rail floor 1.0 ns on ml2 only)
    meas3: slot = m [max(n_c τ_c, t_row) + t_row + n_c τ_c] + 4 FO4
    t_word = drive planes · slot  (bit-serial: 6; ml2: 4)

Fit quality (`a2r/fit.py`): over the seven measured TG + VGS 0.58 points (row_seg 8-32, rf 32-192, sf 16-24), the
law is 0.6-8.8 % slower than ESPice and never faster.

Row reference (grid drive), per plane:

    c_on  = c_w · E[m] / (2^mag − 1) = 1.85 fF        (bit-gated caps; INT4 magnitude stats)
    c_xp  = c_on + pitch · c_wire                       (charge per physical crosspoint)
    α     = c_xp / (rows · a_xp · ρ_MOS · DECAP_FRAC)   a_xp = cell area / (2 · slices); ρ 23.9 fF/µm²; DECAP_FRAC 1.0 (upper bound)
    ε_ref = α · sqrt(rows · p1 (1 − p1)) · (CC_MISMATCH if const_charge else 1),  p1 = sw_activity (bit-serial) | 1/4 (ml2 V level)
    n5 eps_ref ← hypot(eps_ref0, ε_ref)                 (n5 ref_droop term; contract gap marked ponytail)

At the lead α = 1.53 % per row. Without constant charge ε_ref = 2.2 % (34 dB). With constant charge and
CC_MISMATCH 0.03, ε_ref = 0.065 % (63.8 dB alone; 53.5 dB with n5's 0.2 % base). CC_MISMATCH assumes a per-load
5-b dummy trim set from each row's weight sum (the stream-in logic already has the bits) plus MOS-vs-MOM tracking
(projected). With only a fixed dummy the per-row weight spread is about 3.4 % (σ(m)/(E m √512)), and the term
drops to about 50 dB. That costs about −0.02 dB of margin.

Energy per crosspoint per word, added or replaced by the meas modes:

    delivery = planes · act · c_on · V²          act = p1, + (1 − p_hi) with const_charge (bit-serial: 1)
    row_wire = planes · act · pitch · c_wire · V²
    row_switch = planes · cyc · 2 · row_fins · C_gg · V² / row_seg    cyc = act · 4/3 (inverter + FO4 taper) | 2 (ml2 TG)
    share_switch = planes · 3 · share_fins · C_gg · V² / R             (bootstrap ×3; the second event, n9 charges one)
    driver area / weight = 2 · slices / row_seg · sets · row_fins · fin area    sets 2 (+2 dummy) | 6 (ml2)
    dummy area / weight  = 2 · slices · c_xp / ρ_MOS                            (const_charge)

At the lead: τ_c 39.2 ps, τ_r 16.1 ps, casc 0.374 ns, n_c τ_c 0.353 ns. Slot = 1.2 × 2 × 0.374 + 0.04 = 0.94 ns.
Delivery 5.43 fJ, row_switch 1.88 fJ, share_switch 2.11 fJ per crosspoint per word.

**Lead pass energy, 413 pJ** (live): converters 130, n9 buffered reference 137 (n9 charges (1/η − 1) on n2's
delivery), array 69, buffer 37, digital 19, column switches 17, drivers 4.5.

**Transistors and area (lead, per tile, derived).**

- Segment drivers: 8 rows × 128 segments × (32 n + 32 p, main + dummy) = 131k fins, plus 44k in the FO4 taper,
  as about 5.5k devices. Area 383 µm² (1.8 %).
- Constant-charge dummy caps: 735 µm² (3.5 %).
- Column switches: 1,024 × 32 fins = 33k fins, plus 5 bootstrap devices per column; n9 bootstrap area 797 µm².
- Crosspoint TGs (n9 `plate_drive`): 16,384 devices.
- Weight cells: 509k transistors.

So the multiply periphery is about 210k fins, against about 82k for the first A2 lead (recounted on physical
columns) and about 50k for the round-1 pick (O0). As devices it is about 2-3 % of the weight-cell count.
**The "fewer transistors" claim of the first report is withdrawn:** the faster multiply costs about 4× the
periphery fins, and 5.3 % of the tile for drivers plus dummies.

tok/s is not monotonic in speed: the ARCH point is power-capped (1 W/mm²), so a few pJ per pass moves the
operating VDD (0.5 → 0.45 V) and costs more than the 4 % multiply bound. **Under ARCH the tile is now
energy-bound, not time-bound: a faster multiply buys nothing unless it is also cheaper.** The multiply floor
with margin is about 0.84 ns per slot (word 5.9 ns).

## Ceiling (ARCH_METRIC Amdahl)

The round-2 ceiling still holds (critic: upheld): halving the whole tile time gives at most 81.6k under ARCH,
where the prefill attention rail binds, and 0 under Sohu, where the lead is attention-bound. In addition the
ARCH point is now power-capped at 99.6 W, so time alone gives about 0. A gain needs lower pass energy (the
delivery, n9's reference loss on it, and the converters) together with rail_headroom.

  - `plumb`'s buffered reference charges (1/η − 1) on n2's delivery: 137 pJ per pass at the lead, the largest
    single term after the converters. For grid-driven bit-serial rows, the physical loss is the PDN regulator's
    (n9's own pdn note: η 0.5, i.e. ×1). n9 should decide which applies to `inv_bitserial`.
  - n9's `PLATE_DEV` 2 T per plate is consistent with a crosspoint TG and is not double-counted with n2's
    segment drivers. If n9 means a per-plate inverter, E5 shows it is no faster.
  - O4 (the lumped bit-serial law) still scores 61.7k / 14.0 with no row phase and no droop; it should be
    retired in favour of `charge_rail_bps2_cc`.
- **n5:** `eps_ref` is now written by n2 for the meas modes (gap: n5 should read `arr["supply"]["eps"]`).
  row_seg is the main row knob; at seg 8 the row is no longer the bottleneck.
- **n6 (A1):** under the judge's converter (7.88 ns per pass) the multiply should be sized down (O12). With the
  as-scored converter it is 4 % multiply-bound. Converter energy is now the second-largest term, and under ARCH
  energy sets throughput through the power cap.
- **n8:** G2 +0.14 dB at TT; corners need +2.14 dB (SS) and +1.72 dB (FF). bps2's signal-independent top-plate
  injection is still uncredited.
- **n3:** c_on (bit-gated cap) sets the delivery, the droop α and the dummy size, all linearly. A smaller unit
  cap helps all three.
- **n7:** unchanged: Sohu is attention-bound at 72,220 (live).
