# Circuit evidence map for the ten-hour campaign

Archive reviewed 2026-09-10, beginning 22:37 UTC. This is an evidence map and
experiment proposal, not a new transistor result. The root campaign owns the
connected charge-core experiment; the circuit branch owns the proposed isolated
readout experiment. Literature priority and full-model qualification are tracked
separately in [SOTA_EVIDENCE.md](SOTA_EVIDENCE.md) and
[SYSTEM_EVIDENCE.md](SYSTEM_EVIDENCE.md).

The best reproduced physical MAC result is a **128-row by 8-column passive
charge core**, with 0.04277/0.07934 MAC RMS/maximum error at TT/27 °C and
0.10333/0.22338 at SS/85 °C. Its ideal-port delivery is 4.500/4.587 fJ/MAC.
Those results cover three actual activation words with 7, 6, and 5 magnitude
planes, separate corner calibration, fixed coefficient capacitors, and no ADC.
They are not a complete A8/W8 inference engine. The strongest selected physical
ADC remains **failed** on a reserved SS input because its final code is two
codes above the ideal floor. No archived experiment combines the best MAC,
readout, full-model quality, physical weight storage, reference generators,
noise, and extracted area. A Mythic-beating claim is unsupported.

## Evidence protocol and coverage

The authoritative [CONTRACT.md](../CONTRACT.md) was read first. Current evidence
has precedence over superseded status prose. `VERIFIED` below means the named
test and its stated numerical gate, not silicon, yield, or an integrated chip.
`STRONGLY SUPPORTED` denotes independently checked equations or a partial
physical mechanism. `SPECULATIVE` denotes an unbuilt connection. `FAILED`
retains the failed gate even when the mechanism is useful.

The archive inventory records SHA-256, headings, line counts, local document
links, source functions, and assertion counts in
`build/research/campaign_circuit_evidence/inventory.json`.
It covers all **61 root project documents, 15,034 lines**, and **123 selected
analog testbench, schematic, sizing, and layout source files** at the start of
the campaign. This is an inventory, not a claim that every source line was
executed or every old result reproduced. Circuit reports and their controlling
source paths were read in detail; architecture/compiler/system-only documents
were read for circuit interfaces and qualification limits. Existing build
artifacts were inspected selectively. No old result was regenerated by this
map. The root separately performed two fresh baseline simulations.

Failure classes distinguish:

* **Fundamental, within stated model:** charge/noise/energy identities that a
  different sizing point cannot remove.
* **Topology:** an interface, operating sequence, or source impedance violates
  the desired computation.
* **Sizing/timing:** finite settling, headroom, or device loading may be repaired
  without changing the underlying computation.
* **Numerical:** solver failure or a model-port inconsistency; neither a
  physical success nor physical failure follows automatically.
* **Provenance/qualification:** result measured through an ideal interface,
  calibrated with unavailable information, or extrapolated beyond the test.

The physical split DAC contains 756 fF coarse capacitance, 192 fF fine
capacitance, and a 12.8-fF bridge: **960.8 fF physical**. Its coarse port with
the fine top floating has **768 fF effective** capacitance. The prior voltage
acquisition protocol presents **948 fF** before filter and device additions.
These are three different quantities; substituting one for another gives an
incorrect loading or area comparison.

For the existing filter, with `x=Cf/Ch`, the stationary input-referred variance
is `(kT/Ch)(2/x+3+x)`. Its best value at `x=sqrt(2)` is still 177.34 µV RMS
for Ch=768 fF at 300 K. A 100-µV requirement would need Ch≥2.415 pF within
that model. A boxcar average could suppress exchange noise, but 100-µV
residue-referred RMS requires about 13.857 ns and the latch has not been shown
to implement that aperture. More passive filtering alone is therefore a poor
priority unless it is combined with a measured integrating sensing stage.

## Noise and numerical evidence must stay separate

Ordinary pinned ngspice transient analysis includes deterministic BSIM physics
but not intrinsic transistor noise. `TRNOISE` is an explicit source and a
stationary `.noise` calculation is a linearization; neither converts a quiet
transient waveform into a dynamic-noise proof.

The [BSIM audit](../IMC_BSIM_NOISE_AUDIT.md) and
[transient-noise path](../IMC_TRANSIENT_NOISE_PATH.md) establish useful tools:

* A pinned VACASK/OpenVAF path reproduces an RC noise check: 240,004 points,
  1.287-dB PSD discrepancy within the declared 3-dB gate, and variance
  1.00656 kT/C. Zero-noise replay gives zero variance.
* Native BSIM4.5 and BSIM4.8 have materially different `tnoiMod=1` equations.
  Their GHz noise ratios vary with device and bias, so a global noise gain is
  invalid. A documented 4.8-electrical/4.5-noise hybrid reproduces stationary
  legacy spectra to 1.31 ppm at 21 TT/SS operating points; it is not a full
  BSIM4.5 transient port.
* A 438-point TT/SS DC audit validates the repaired electrical model fields.
  The native 4.5 charge Jacobian has some 2.46% discrepancies; actual charge
  curves agree with the repaired derivatives. This matters for transient
  loading and for claimed device-port capacitance.
* A nine-MOS comparator transient migration passes TT/SS deterministic
  waveform, energy, and decision checks. Unit noise originally fails at the
  first 41.667-ps stochastic boundary. A documented SDE tolerance of 30 runs
  two seeds and signs, while smaller settings fail. This is progress in
  numerical capability, **not** a converged input-referred sigma or BER result.

The long 1-pF/1-ms scalar-log run aborts at 0.016503 s at TT and SS. An isolated
TT variant with explicit closing nonoverlap aborts at the same instant. Keep
that result **numerical nonconvergence**. It neither proves the product wrong
nor licenses extrapolating an uncompleted run. The diagnostic preserves old
logs; see [gm/ID round](../IMC_LOG_GMID_ROUND.md).

## Area, model quality, and implementation boundaries

The layout tree contains device/tap wrappers and technology traits, not actual
MAC, ADC, or capacitor layout generators. Sky130 MOS length dispatch implements
150 nm only; the 300/500/1000-nm paths are unimplemented. There is no current
extracted-layout/DRC/LVS result supporting the analog architecture. A MOM
parameter structure is not a verified 4-fF capacitor implementation.

The [capacitor sizing study](../IMC_CAPACITOR_SIZING.md) uses explicit assumed
matching coefficients, rather than a Sky130 MOM yield characterization.
Nominal MIM density 2 fF/µm² is not a MOM matching law, and the cited minimum
MIM geometry implies roughly 8 fF. Thus the 4-fF unit remains a layout question.
The noise/mismatch study usefully shows that aggressive redundant tiny-cap
calibration can cost more than simply using larger physical capacitance.
An assumed .598-mV read noise and a 27-µV characterization target need roughly
490 independent measurements; static error does not average down.

The full-model record prevents selecting a falsely cheap circuit target:

* Naive W4 already approximately doubles perplexity versus the Q8 reference
  before analog errors. A sparse W4 circuit cannot silently stand in for two
  denser signed-magnitude W8 slices.
* Blockwise A8 normalizing to peak 127 normally uses seven magnitude planes;
  the selected three-word six-plane average is not a general latency law.
* The direct radix full-model experiment fails its joint quality gate at
  R128, 100-µV read noise, and a 12-bit/.5-V converter on the tested slices.
  Later smoothing/A9/R256 configurations improve the frontier but retain a
  held-out failure. Read noise is a system objective, not an optional detail.
* Readout sharing changes ADC instances and scheduling, not the number of
  services unless a physically legal reduction is performed before conversion.
  Pooling different outputs or independently scaled activations is invalid.
* Compiler-only mapping and protected-plane experiments are useful controls,
  but their saved activation/noise boundaries must match the claimed error
  mechanism. One protected layer dramatically helps independent output noise
  and barely helps static weight mismatch.

### Experiment 1: physical CDAC state reuse, then real conversion

Root owns tb_imc_connected_core.py.
For coarse unit `u`, coarse capacitance is `63u`, fine capacitance `16u`, and
bridge `16u/15`. With the fine top floating, effective top capacitance is
`64u+C0`. Choose `C0=CA−64u` only after including filter and settled device
loads in the holder budget. With fixed `u` across columns, a fine step injects
`u ΔV/16`, so both signal and reference are divided by the same CA:

\[
\frac{h_B}{\Delta V_\mathrm{LSB}}=
\frac{16 C_u V_s}{u\Delta V\,2^B}S.
\]

This allows a common charge LSB while total matching capacitance varies with
the programmed column. It does not make the maximum physical matching bank,
its programming cells, or its parasitics disappear. The split network costs
16.0667u more physical capacitance than its effective top load. Two pipeline
banks must both be counted. Generic compute-capacitor SAR reuse is known prior
art; the test concerns this exact recurrence and interface.

The root's initial integration results are already informative: ideal VCM
bottoms plus a real fine reset pass column 0 at .009906/.014507 MAC RMS/max.
Adding 0.42-µm reference TGs fails at 5.017663/5.870666; adding the filter and a
reset-state comparator still fails at 3.915408/4.639534. The largest coarse
bottom is 6.509 mV from VCM just before sharing ends, with 1.553-mV RMS across
planes. Therefore the holder is a frequency-dependent network during sharing,
not just its DC Schur-complement capacitance.

Discriminating experiments are independent bottom-TG width and sharing-time
sweeps, and a smaller active CDAC with a permanently connected matching
capacitor. Save bottom voltages and integrate each reference-source current
over sharing. Compare comparator held in reset versus active after the same
actual MAC endpoint; retain the seven untouched columns as controls. Freeze
calibration before new words/corners and keep the existing .25/1-MAC analog
gate separate from the converter's code-accuracy gate. A code is not a MAC.
Do not reinterpret externally imposed bits as comparator decisions.

### Experiment 2: an integrating preamp with isolated regeneration

The minimal candidate is an NMOS differential pair with PMOS drain precharge,
a physically biased and switched tail, and a separate StrongARM receiving its
drain voltages. A sensing interval develops differential voltage first; the
tail then turns off before regeneration. Initial tests keep the external
signal on the preamp gates, avoiding an additional input sampling switch and
its reset noise. This is not perfect isolation: latch motion can still couple
through the preamp's nonlinear gate-drain capacitance, and must be measured.

For an ideal constant-current sensing interval T, per-device transconductance
gm and output capacitance Co, first-order differential gain is

\[
A_v\simeq\frac{g_m T}{C_o}.
\]

Finite output resistance modifies the gain to
`gm·ro·(1−exp(−T/(ro Co)))`. Common-mode output falls by approximately
`ID T/Co` per branch; it must leave headroom for both preamp input devices and
the subsequent latch. This common-mode constraint prevents choosing arbitrary
gain and arbitrarily small Co.

Using **one-sided** channel-current PSD `4 kT gamma gm` for each independent
input device, the integrated differential output variance is
`4 kT gamma gm T/Co²`. Dividing by the squared differential gain gives

\[
\sigma_{v,\mathrm{pair}}^2\simeq
\frac{4\gamma kT}{g_m T}.
\]

The earlier informal factor-two estimate was optimistic; the expression above
uses gm of one input transistor, two independent input channel-noise sources,
and differential output. For gamma=2/3 at 300 K, a 100-µV pair-only allocation
requires gmT≈1.104 pF. At gm/ID=20 V⁻¹ and VDD=1.8 V, pair current alone gives

\[
E_\mathrm{pair}\simeq
2V_{DD}\frac{g_mT}{g_m/I_D}\approx199\ \mathrm{fJ/trial}.
\]

This is a screening estimate, not a BSIM noise result or a universal lower
bound. Reset noise, tail noise, flicker noise, finite sensing bandwidth,
common-mode motion, and latch noise remain. The estimate shows that a very
quiet preamp may cost more than the current comparator's roughly 50 fJ/trial;
its architectural value would have to come from fewer complete conversions
or a corrected error frontier, not from asserting free noise suppression.

The first actual-device test should size with existing tables and independently
measure actual gm/ID, gds/ID, gate-port admittance, and charge at the chosen
source/body/drain bias. Use a real charged 304–768-fF endpoint, the existing
floating/stiff comparator as a matched baseline, and a near-threshold input
set including the failing SS polarity. Measure gain, held-node disturbance,
decision sign, supply-positive energy through closing reset, and residual
charge after the next cycle. All physical bias sources are counted. A direct
latch and a latch-disabled control separate regeneration kickback from preamp
sampling effects.

Once deterministic sensing works, inject small charge impulses at different
times to measure the actual decision sensitivity kernel. Only then may the
filter-noise covariance be integrated against that kernel or a qualified
dynamic device-noise simulation be interpreted as an input sigma. A 12-ns
waiting interval cannot be relabeled as noise averaging. Offset calibration
must be explicit and frozen; random device mismatch is not covered by TT/SS.

### Radical branch's cheapest falsifier

For a source-modulated stored-weight cell, measure DC current `I(Vw,Va)` at
fixed real column compliance over the intended operand window and both
corners. A separable product requires the cross-ratio

\[
R=\frac{I(V_w,V_a)I(V_{w0},V_{a0})}
{I(V_w,V_{a0})I(V_{w0},V_a)}-1
\]

to be small. This eliminates arbitrary individual weight gains and row input
gains and exposes the mixed derivative of log current. A large R cannot be
repaired by two independent one-dimensional calibrations. Source sensitivity
includes `−(gm+gmb+gds)/I`, not merely `−gm/I`. For capacitive row injection,
`beta=Cin/(Cs+Cin+Cgate)` is itself voltage-dependent; the row swing and cell
area must include Cin. Body modulation needs an actual isolated well and its
area, not a free per-row substrate voltage. After DC separability, physically
program a cell, pulse the row, return it, and score read-current drift as well
as stored voltage. The first DC experiment belongs to the prior-art branch.
